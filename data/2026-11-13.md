### [1] [Measuring, Revealing and Mitigating Harmful Content Propagation in Recommender Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15160)
*Xiaokun Zhang, Youlin Wu, Junyu Lu, Jiamin Chen, Zhaochun Ren, Hongfei Lin, Fenglong Ma, Xue Liu, Chen Ma*

### [2] [When Direction Matters: Diagnosing Relational Instruction Following in Dense Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15417)
*Zhanyu Wu, Richong Zhang*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 向量检索/Vector Retrieval | Importance: 中

TL;DR: 提出 SwitchIR 诊断基准，发现在关系方向反转（Relational Flip）时稠密检索器与 late-interaction 检索器普遍失效；通过精确的 margin 分解指出：仅当方向敏感的 query-doc 交互超过方向无关的成对偏好时，双向才同时正确。

### [3] [SurvEval: Listwise Re-Ranking Evaluator via Survival-Based Exposure and Multi-Objective Value Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15434)
*YaChen Yan, Liubo Li*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 重排模型/Reranking | Importance: 中

TL;DR: 提出 SurvEval，一种面向重排阶段的列表级评估器（offline evaluator）：将用户顺序浏览过程建模为离散时间生存模型以导出单调递减的“到达概率”，并结合多目标价值估计（多头预测 + 基于不确定性的损失加权），最终以位置级“到达概率 × 条件价值”求和得到列表效用。

Ranking Relevance: 与重排强相关但切入点是“评估器”而非生成器：重排的效果长期受困于离线指标（如 NDCG、单目标 pCTR 加和）与线上多目标收益不一致，SurvEval 直接针对这一痛点，把位置折扣替换为可学习的生存概率、把标量效用替换为多目标价值场，可作为重排/多样性打散/序列生成式重排模型的离线评估器与训练奖励信号（类似 listwise reward model）。工业价值在于：可用于重排策略的离线对比与超参选择、降低 A/B 依赖；局限是对曝光日志的级联假设与数据集规模敏感，且未涉及生成式重排的动作空间与约束，跨场景迁移仍待验证。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有重排评估器存在两个简化：(1) 对用户浏览行为假设过于简单（如独立的曝光/点击假设、固定的位置折扣），无法刻画浏览的级联衰减结构；(2) 将多目标信号坍缩为单一标量，忽视了多目标间的尺度与噪声差异，导致评估结果无法反映真实的列表级（listwise）效用。

We propose \textbf{SurvEval}, a listwise evaluator that jointly models user exposure and item value for re-ranking evaluation. We formalize the sequential browsing process as a discrete-time survival model, deriving monotonically decreasing reaching probabilities that capture the cascading structure of user browsing. We further introduce a multi-objective value estimation module with dedicated per-objective prediction heads and uncertainty-based loss weighting to adaptively balance objectives of varying scales and noise levels. Both components are jointly optimized via a unified transformer backbone, and the listwise utility is obtained by summing the position-wise products of reaching probabilities and conditional item value estimates. Extensive experiments on KuaiRand and Avito demonstrate that SurvEval achieves superior performance over state-of-the-art baselines.
