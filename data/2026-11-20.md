### [1] [LSP-RAG: Latent Scoring Perspectives for Knowledge Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25229)

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 其他/Other | Importance: 低

This design allows LSP-RAG to perform accurate and robust retrieval using lightweight language models.
