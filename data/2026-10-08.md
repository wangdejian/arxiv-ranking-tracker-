### [1] [Trustworthy Domain-Specific AI for Structured Knowledge Retrieval and Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08894)
*Ryan C. Barron*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 博士论文提出一套可扩展架构，将领域非结构化文本转化为结构化知识以支持检索与推理：包括 Binary Bleed（加速 NMF 低秩搜索）、HNMFk（深度自适应主题建模、可解释分类体系）、类型化知识图谱与语义对齐向量库（事件驱动同步）、T-SRAG（张量结构化 RAG，动态路由检索路径，对比对齐减少幻觉）以及基于张量的链接预测补全图谱。

Ranking Relevance: 与搜推广排序模型为间接相关：T-SRAG 的多路径动态路由、对比对齐的层级主题嵌入，与多路召回/多目标融合、query-doc 表示学习思路相通；HNMFk 自动隐特征选择可启发特征分组与主题特征构造；张量链接预测与图召回（如 item-item 图、知识图谱增强召回）相关。但论文未涉及 CTR/CVR 预估、特征交叉、精排/粗排建模，不直接提升排序效果；其价值更多在于为召回侧提供可解释、抗幻觉的领域知识表示与检索层，可作为排序模型的上游知识源或辅助监督信号。

These representations populate a typed Knowledge Graph and a semantically aligned Vector Store containing extracted latent features, synchronized through an event-driven substrate.
  Tensor-Structured Retrieval-Augmented Generation (T-SRAG) dynamically routes queries across retrieval paths. Contrastive alignment maps document and query embeddings to hierarchical topic structures to improve semantic fidelity and reduce hallucinations. Beyond retrieval, tensor-based link prediction identifies and completes missing links in the Knowledge Graph, supporting inference grounded in citation structure.
  Applications across cybersecurity, law, materials science, and healthcare demonstrate improvements in retrieval precision, early trend detection, hypothesis generation, and hallucination mitigation. The dissertation provides a deployable, modular foundation for trustworthy, domain-specific AI systems that retrieve and reason over structured knowledge.

### [2] [What Transfers from a VLM Teacher? Comparing Supervision Signals for Visual Document Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09177)
*Saba Sturua, Han Xiao*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 中

TL;DR: 该论文研究视觉文档检索（VDR）中VLM教师信号的更优使用方式：将教师用于评判检索器挖掘出的难负样本，而非增强正样本，从而显著提升检索nDCG。

Ranking Relevance: 该工作针对多模态（视觉文档）检索器的对比学习训练，属于召回/向量检索范畴，但其中"用教师模型评判挖掘负样本、纠正假负样本"的思想对排序/预估模型的负采样与知识蒸馏有直接借鉴价值。其核心结论——标签不完整导致的假负样本是训练瓶颈，教师信号用于负样本侧比正样本侧更有效——可迁移到搜推广中的双塔召回、粗排负采样及LLM-as-judge的样本标注/去噪流程。对工业系统而言，提供了低成本提升检索质量的蒸馏范式与开源教师判断数据。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统对比式视觉文档检索训练中，每个query仅有一个标注正样本，负样本被默认视为不相关，但标签本身极不完整。近期方法把VLM教师蒸馏到编码器时主要用于丰富正样本（如注意力接地、描述对齐）。作者质疑：教师信号用在挖掘出的候选（尤其是被当作负样本的页面）上是否更有价值，因为这些候选标签缺失，可能是被错误判为负的相关页面。

Method: 在固定student、数据、优化器和评测的前提下，对比几种教师信号使用方式：(1) teacher-judged hard negatives（教师评判难负样本）；(2) score distillation（分数蒸馏）；(3) description alignment（描述对齐）；(4) attention grounding（注意力接地）。同时与同样训练算力下、从同一挖掘池中筛选四候选的无教师规则（含当前系统使用的正样本感知阈值）对比。使教师通过贪婪解码的0-100打分提示进行相关/不相关判别，并做十人标注审计验证教师可靠性。

Result: ViDoRe v2 nDCG@5从55.2提升至62.6（教师评判难负）和63.0（分数蒸馏）；描述对齐仅+2.6，注意力接地无明显增益。相比无教师规则，教师判断在v2上额外+4.1、v3上+1.7。标注审计显示，query前四的挖掘候选中约两个实为相关但均未标注；82%的教师评分落在量表两端，二值相关/不相关划分即可保留大部分蒸馏增益。十人审计表明教师处于人类标注者变异范围内，在单一确定答案的query上可靠。

Conclusion: 在视觉文档检索中，VLM教师的价值主要体现在对挖掘负样本进行评判和分数蒸馏，而非增强正样本；这源于对比标签的不完整性。教师信号虽粗糙（近似二值），但可靠且带来稳定增益，作者开源了代码、教师判断、页面描述、挖掘池、人工审计和适配器。

Abstract: Visual document retrievers are trained contrastively: each query is matched to one page labelled relevant - the positive - and pushed away from negatives, pages presumed irrelevant. Recent methods distil a vision-language model (VLM) teacher into the retriever by enriching that positive, transferring the teacher's attention over it or a description of it. We ask whether the teacher is better spent on the other side, judging the candidates the retriever mines as negatives, which the label says nothing about. With student, data, optimizer and evaluation fixed, teacher-judged hard negatives and score distillation raise ViDoRe v2 nDCG@5 from 55.2 to 62.6 and 63.0; description alignment, as adapted here, gains 2.6 points and attention grounding nothing measurable. Against teacher-free rules that select four candidates from the same mined pool at identical training compute, the best of which is the positive-aware threshold current systems use, the teacher's judgement adds 4.1 points on v2 and 1.7 on v3. This is consistent with how incomplete the labels are. Annotators judge about two of a query's four top-ranked mined candidates relevant, none of them labelled, so training pushes the retriever away from relevant pages treated as negatives. What reaches the student is coarse: under a greedily decoded 0-100 rating prompt, 82% of the teacher's ratings come back at one end of the scale or the other, and a relevant/irrelevant partition keeps most of the distillation gain. A ten-annotator audit places the teacher within the range of variation among human annotators, and finds it reliable where a query has a single determinate answer. We release the code, the teacher's 3.3M judgements and page descriptions, the mined pools, the human audit and the trained adapters at https://github.com/elastic/vdr-teacher-signals.

### [3] [Quantize by Drift: Label-Free Mixed-Precision Post-Training Quantization for Text Embedders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09227)
*Hyojung Han, Jongmin Kim, Seung-Hun Jeon*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 向量检索/Vector Retrieval | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 提出一种无标签的量化敏感度信号：量化单个模块后重新编码语料，测量输出embedding相对全精度位置的漂移（representation drift）。该漂移在配置层面能以宏观Spearman 0.911排序混合精度方案与留出检索质量，但作为分配目标仅优于基线、不如等精度均匀量化的绝对质量。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 混合精度训练后量化（PTQ）需要逐模块敏感度信号来分配比特。对文本embedder而言，最直接的敏感度指标是量化某模块导致的检索质量损失，但这依赖相关性标签，实际部署中往往没有。因此需要一种无标签的替代信号。

Method: 以"输出表示漂移"作为可观测代理指标：量化单个模块后重新编码语料，记录输出embedding相对全精度位置的偏移，在硬性打包字节预算下做一次加性比特分配，无需标签、无需搜索。在5个开发用embedder上评测配置级漂移与留出检索质量的排序一致性，并在3个封存的embedder上做预注册方向性假设检验，对比LieQ准则与同预算均匀精度。

Abstract: Mixed-precision post-training quantization needs a per-module sensitivity signal; for a text embedder the obvious one -- the retrieval quality a module costs when quantized -- needs relevance labels that deployments rarely have. What is specific is the observable: the deployed output representation a dense retriever ranks with. Across five development embedders, configuration-level drift orders sampled mixed-precision plans against held-out retrieval quality at a macro Spearman of 0.911, the sensitivity transports across calibration corpora and retrieval domains in the usable regime, module drifts compose rank-consistently but not numerically, and relevance-derived sensitivity adds no consistent value. The method is one additive allocation under a hard packed-byte budget, with no labels and no search. On three embedders held untouched until method, baselines and hypotheses were frozen and sealed, the pre-registered directional hypothesis against the prior LieQ criterion holds (3/3 at the main budget, no collapse) and drift scores above a two-sided LieQ steelman in 2/3; but at the main budget drift is numerically lower than same-budget uniform precision on all three (-0.99, -0.85, -1.01 points), having reduced module and whole-model drift as designed. Output drift is thus a robust coarse sensitivity signal, not a universally optimal allocation objective: it avoids the catastrophic failures of the transferred signed-geometry adaptation and can remain usable at stressed budgets where uniform collapses, but fine-grained redistribution around a strong uniform operating point remains unresolved.

Abstract: Retrieval-augmented generation systems increasingly rely on document-structure treatments: structure-aligned chunking, LLM-generated chunk contexts, heading-path metadata, and hierarchical two-stage retrieval. Separate studies support each on different corpora, embedders, and metrics, and none control for a shared confound: any text prepended to a chunk perturbs its embedding. We present a mechanism-isolating ablation testing all four treatments under one protocol, matching chunk sizes across conditions and adding a semantically null placebo---heading paths that are structurally valid but shuffled across documents. We score retrieval with a coverage-aware nDCG and test four pre-registered contrasts via document-clustered bootstrap with Holm correction, on two distant corpora: 200 Wikipedia Featured Articles (951 queries) and 1,585 QASPER papers (4,303 questions). Organization helps, and the cause is content, not tokens: structure-aligned chunks with real heading paths beat contextualized fixed windows (+0.022 / +0.012 cov-nDCG@10) and the placebo (+0.010 / +0.016). Naive two-stage hierarchical retrieval hurts (-0.033 / -0.015), traceable to first-stage section recall. Gold structure beats LLM-induced structure on Wikipedia but not on QASPER. Effects are small ($dz$ 0.06-0.11) but Holm-significant and consistent across corpora.
