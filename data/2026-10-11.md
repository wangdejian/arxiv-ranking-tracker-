### [1] [LIFT: A Lifecycle-aware Interaction Factorization Transformer for Unified Retrieval and Ranking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10556)
*Keji Miao, Enhao Cheng, Yan Li, Qun Li, Qi Zhang, Jie Yuan, Xiaoyong Li*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 推荐系统/Recommender System | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 提出LIFT（Lifecycle-aware Interaction Factorization Transformer），将用户交互拆解为Request/Item/Context/Action有序状态并建模为因果序列，使召回读取Request状态、排序读取Context状态，实现共享历史建模同时保留各阶段专属信息。

Ranking Relevance: 直接相关排序模型：论文核心是同一序列建模骨干下的召回-排序联合建模，通过状态级读位（Request给召回、Context给排序）解决两阶段信息不一致问题。其Role-Conditioned Attention与Pre-LN Bias可直接迁移到工业级级联架构中，用于统一序列特征、降低多阶段重复建模成本，并为Joint Score（联合指标）类型的端到端优化提供参考。

Motivation: 级联推荐系统中召回与排序复用同一份用户历史，但两阶段在一次交互中所处的信息位点与可用信息不同，直接共享历史会引入阶段无关或阶段不适配的信息，导致建模次优。

Loss-weight sweeps show favorable retrieval--ranking trade-offs, while ablations and scaling analyses further examine lifecycle sequence construction, model components, and capacity settings.

  <summary>Details</summary>
