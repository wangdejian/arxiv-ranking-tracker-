### [1] [After One-Click Video: The Inflow of Service-Type Content, the Failure of the Recommendation Pool Mechanism, and the BoLe System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37695)

### [4] [Relevance-Guided Residual Calibration for Spectral Compression of Dense Retrieval Embeddings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37707)
*Yongkang Li, Panagiotis Eustratiadis, Evangelos Kanoulas*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 向量检索/Vector Retrieval | Importance: 中/Medium

Ranking Relevance: 属于召回侧（稠密向量检索/索引压缩）工作，而非打分排序模型本身：其价值在于降低召回阶段的存储与在线打分成本，并用标注数据做轻量监督校准。对工业搜推广的意义：①为 embedding 降维（如 768→256/128）提供一种离线闭式、无需重训双塔、不改在线 serving 的增益手段，可直接嵌入现有 ANN 索引构建流程；②与 PQ/标量量化正交，可在同一存储预算下提升召回上限，进而抬高粗排/精排的候选质量；③局限是依赖相关性标注构造正负样本、且只优化内积/余弦空间的各向权重，不涉及交叉特征与多任务排序，对精排模型本身无直接影响；④需注意其"正负样本分布"与线上真实分布一致性，否则校准权重可能过拟合离线标注。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 谱压缩（投影到主子空间）可降低稠密检索的存储与打分开销，但其权重完全由语料谱（谱能量/方差趋势）决定，把所有保留方向绑定在一条共享的谱衰减趋势上，没有直接建模各方向对"相关性判别"的单独贡献。作者希望在不改变压缩框架、不增加在线成本的前提下，引入标注相关性信号对方向权重做个性化修正。

Method: 1) 对每个保留方向计算正负样本的得分贡献（投影分量）均值与方差，构造 margin-based 权重 w_d = (μ+ − μ−)^2 / (σ+^2 + σ−^2) 一类间隔统计量；2) 仅保留 w_d > 0 的方向作为支撑集；3) 将 log w_d 拟合为幂律趋势（log d 的线性回归）+ 残差；4) 把残差加到原压缩器的 log 权重上，从而既保留压缩器整体谱衰减形状，又让标注对各方向做局部调整；5) 修正合并进原有的同一变换矩阵，文档与查询共享，因而不改变推理流程，也无迭代训练。适用于 PCA / Whitening / SpecTemp。

Result: 3 个编码器 × 9 个数据集（BEIR + BRIGHT，含推理密集型检索）：在全部目标维度上 SpecTemp + RRC 的平均效果最佳；BRIGHT 5 个数据集在 768 维平均提升 2.34 nDCG@10；增益在标量量化与乘积量化下依然保持，改善了"每文档存储—效果"权衡。

Conclusion: 语料谱决定的压缩权重并非最优，相关性标注可在不改变压缩结构的前提下提供方向级修正；"保留谱趋势 + 残差校准"是一种低成本、闭式、可插拔的方案，并与量化压缩正交叠加。

Abstract: Spectral compression reduces the storage and scoring costs of dense retrieval by projecting embeddings onto a smaller principal subspace. However, its corpus-derived weighting rules tie all retained directions to a shared spectral trend, without directly accounting for their individual contributions to relevance. To address this, we propose Relevance-Guided Residual Calibration (RRC), a closed-form method for refining spectral weights using relevance judgments. RRC estimates margin-based weights from the means and variances of positive--negative score contributions along each retained direction. It retains directions with positive estimated weights, decomposes their log weights into a fitted power-law trend and a residual, and adds the residual to the compressor's log weights on this support. This allows relevance judgments to adjust individual directions while preserving the compressor's overall spectral trend. RRC applies to PCA, Whitening, and SpecTemp, and incorporates the corrections into the existing transformation shared by documents and queries, without iterative model training or an additional inference stage. Experiments with three encoders on nine datasets covering conventional and reasoning-intensive retrieval show that SpecTemp + RRC achieves the highest mean effectiveness among tested compressors on the BEIR group and BRIGHT at every target dimension. On the five evaluated BRIGHT datasets, it improves over SpecTemp by an average of 2.34 nDCG@10 points at 768 dimensions. The gains also persist under scalar and product quantization, improving the trade-off between per-document storage and retrieval effectiveness. Our code is available at: https://anonymous.4open.science/r/rrc-spectral-compression-B5CE/
