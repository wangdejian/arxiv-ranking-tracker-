### [1] [Towards High-Performance Multimodal Discovery: Evaluating Retrieval Efficacy and Operational Scalability in Digital Libraries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17726)
*Krishna Priya, Sai Navya Jyesta, Sachith Withana, Neelesh Karthikeyan, Beth Plale*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 向量检索/Vector Retrieval | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 该文针对百万级数字化文献库的多模态发现难题，提出用VLM将页面图像（含文本与图表）编码为数值向量替代OCR全文倒排索引，评估多模态检索的语义精度与架构可扩展性，发现视觉+文本特征融合显著提升检索效果，并在优化向量数据库配置后实现亚线性扩展、达到生产可用规模。

Ranking Relevance: 该文属召回侧（向量召回/多模态检索）工作，而非排序模型本身：其核心是候选生成阶段用VLM页面级embedding替代OCR倒排检索，为后续精排提供更高质量的候选池。对搜推广系统的启示有三：一是文档型/内容型场景（文档、商品图文、短视频封面）可用页面级或图文联合embedding统一建模多模态语义；二是验证了"亚线性扩展+向量库配置调优"是工业落地的关键，与ANN索引参数、分片、量化等工程实践直接相关；三是未涉及LTR/CTR预估或特征交叉，与排序模型相关性间接，价值主要在于召回架构与多模态表征输入侧。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 大型数字图书馆藏量达百万卷以上，传统倒排索引（Lucene/Solr）对文本字符串可扩展但"看不见"图像语义，无法理解图表、标注图与复杂版式；同时OCR纯文本化会丢失视觉语义，并存在版权与逆向工程风险。

Method: 以页面图像为单位，用视觉语言模型（VLM）将页面上的文本与图像统一编码为数值向量（替代OCR抽取文本），构建多模态向量检索系统；重点评估向量数据库配置优化，并从语义精度与架构可扩展性两个维度对比多模态检索与传统全文检索。

Result: 融合视觉与文本特征的检索显著提升相关材料发现效果，在图表对概念理解至关重要的领域尤为明显；系统检索性能和扩展性呈亚线性，向量数据库的调优配置是将VLM embedding转化为生产级图书馆规模方案的关键因素。

Conclusion: 多模态向量检索可有效替代/补充倒排索引用于大规模文献发现；向量库工程配置（而非仅embedding模型本身）是决定工业级可用性的核心变量。

Abstract: Large-scale digital repositories with million plus digitized volumes serve as vital engines for scientific and scholarly discovery. However, as collections grow, finding specific research or educational material remains a significant challenge. This difficulty is compounded by the multimodal nature of texts, that is, texts which combine dense narrative content with essential illustrative charts, annotated diagrams, and complex layouts. Historically, discovery has been supported by full-text search indexes implemented as inverted indexes (e.g., Lucene or Solr). While these systems scale well for textual strings, they are fundamentally "blind" to the semantic nuance of visual information. We explore the benefit of discovery through texts that have been converted to numerical vectors. By representing page images as numerical vectors rather than OCR-extracted text, and doing so for both text and images on a page, we capture both textual and visual semantics while mitigating reverse engineering and copyright infringement concerns. The primary contribution of this research is an evaluation of the multimodal retrieval landscape, encompassing both semantic precision and architectural scalability. Our findings demonstrate that integrating visual and textual features significantly enhances the discovery of relevant material, particularly in domains where diagrammatic content is essential for conceptual understanding. We assess retrieval efficacy, demonstrating that the system achieves sub-linear scalability. Our findings suggest that an optimized vector database configuration is the critical factor in transforming VLM-based embeddings into a production-ready, library-scale discovery solution.

### [2] [Who Counts as Exposed? Measurement Choices in an Open, Re-estimable Index of Occupational AI Exposure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18168)
*Luca Mouchel, Pierre Bouquet, Yossi Sheffi*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 其他/Other | Importance: 低/Low

TL;DR: 构建开放可重估的职业—任务级AI暴露指数，通过变化"上下文增强"与"校准"两个设计选择，量化这些选择对暴露度结论的影响，并发现校准对排名与分布的影响大于上下文。

Ranking Relevance: 该文属劳动经济学/技术影响评估，非搜推广排序方法论。但其"LLM-as-judge + 检索上下文 + 集成校准"的评测框架与排序中的LLM标注、标签校准、排序一致性评估有方法学共鸣：其"上下文只平移分数水平、不改善排序"的结论，对RAG式特征增强是否提升排序AUC有参照意义；"校准锚定单一平台用户会引入偏差"亦对应工业中位置偏差/样本选择偏差的校准风险。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有职业AI暴露指数（如2023基准）被用于经济分析与政策制定，但其设计选择不透明、不可重估，导致"哪些工人被判定为暴露"高度依赖主观设定。作者希望揭示这些设计选择的敏感性。

Method: 对18796个职业—任务对，用7个开放权重推理模型按统一rubric打分；每模型分"有/无上下文"（检索到的新闻与研究段落、职场描述、瓶颈式推理协议）两种条件；集成结果分"有/无单纯形约束校准"（对齐Claude实际使用数据）两臂，共四种组合，并与2023基准对比。

Result: 与基准的最大分歧不在"AI能做什么"，而在"对依赖尚未建成的软件集成的暴露该给多少权重"，这是数据无法裁决的选择；按基准权重，校准指数暴露人数更少，仅计直接暴露时更多；全分辨率下集成排名对实际使用和独立物理任务分类的判别优于基准；上下文主要改变暴露水平而非排序；上下文与校准都将暴露向高薪职业偏移，校准影响更大。

Conclusion: 暴露指数作为政策输入时应披露设计选择、在替代设定下报告数值，并随系统能力变化重估；同时发布未校准版本以保证可复现与可争议性。

Abstract: Occupational AI exposure indices are used as inputs to economic analysis, workforce planning, and policy, and the choices built into them decide which workers are described as exposed. We build an open, re-estimable task-level index and vary its two main design decisions, context and calibration, to show how much the answer depends on them. For each of the 18,796 occupation--task pairs in \onet, seven open-weight reasoning models rate exposure under a common rubric. Each model rates with or without a context of retrieved news and research passages, workplace descriptors, and a bottleneck-based reasoning protocol, and the ensemble is combined with or without simplex-constrained calibration to observed Claude usage. The largest difference from the dominant 2023 benchmark is not a disagreement about what AI can do but about how much credit to give exposure that depends on software integrations not yet built, a choice that no data can settle. At the benchmark's own weight, the calibrated index counts fewer exposed workers than the benchmark, and when only direct exposure counts it counts more. At full score resolution, the ensemble ranks tasks better than the benchmark against observed usage and an independent physical-task classification. Adding context mainly shifts the level of exposure rather than improving the ranking. Context and calibration both relocate exposure toward higher-wage occupations, and calibration, which anchors the index to one platform's users, does more of it, so we also release the uncalibrated arm. Exposure indices used as policy inputs should state such choices, report their numbers under the alternatives, and be re-estimated as systems change.

### [3] [Explanations that Transfer: Interpreting Medical Retrieval with Sparse AutoEncoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18179)
*Diane Bouchacourt, Gaëtan Brison*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 中

TL;DR: 将稀疏自编码器（SAE）应用于医疗检索：把稠密文本embedding转成可解释稀疏码，训练目标为重构损失+InfoNCE对比损失以保留query-doc相关性；在3个embedding模型、6个医疗检索数据集上，域内检索优于原embedding，域外性能衰减更小，且特征语义可从激活的query/doc反推。

Ranking Relevance: 属于向量召回/稠密检索侧的工作：直接作用于双塔embedding与相似度计算。对工业召回系统的价值在于（1）稀疏化embedding可解释检索行为、辅助badcase归因与embedding审计；（2）对比损失约束下的SAE有正则效果，缓解领域微调的过拟合，提升跨域（跨病种/跨语料）召回鲁棒性；（3）稀疏码天然可做倒排/稀疏索引，与稀疏-稠密混合召回架构有结合空间。局限是未涉及排序阶段特征交叉与精排模型，且检索指标提升幅度与推理开销（SAE前向+稀疏维度）需在工业QPS约束下验证。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 医疗问答依赖大规模文档检索，领域微调虽能提升相关性，但稠密embedding缺乏可解释性；同时针对特定医疗语料适配可能损害对新临床语料的迁移能力。作者希望同时解决"可解释"与"跨域泛化"两个问题。

Method: 在稠密embedding上训练SAE，得到稀疏可解释表示。损失=重构损失（保真原表示）+InfoNCE对比损失（直接对齐query-document相关性，而非仅重构doc侧）。跨3个embedding模型、6个医疗检索数据集（域内+域外）评估检索效果、迁移性与特征可解释性。

Result: 训练于医疗语料后，稀疏码在域内检索上超过原始稠密embedding；域外检索性能下降幅度小于稠密基线，且目标语料与训练语料差异越大优势越明显。特征具有可解释语义，可通过激活最强的query/doc推断含义，从而解释"为何召回该文档"，且该可解释性可跨训练分布迁移。

Conclusion: SAE+对比目标是稠密检索的可解释化与正则化手段，兼具域内增益和域外鲁棒性；为检索系统的可解释性分析与embedding压缩/稀疏化提供了一条低成本插件式路径。

Abstract: Medical AI systems that answer users' queries rely on retrieving medical knowledge from large document collections. In this context, finetuning general models on medical datasets can improve retrieval relevance. Yet the dense embeddings underlying modern retrieval systems remain largely opaque, making it difficult to understand the mechanics behind their results. Moreover, adapting to a specific medical dataset risks losing transferability to new clinical corpora. In this work, we tackle these issues by applying Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs) to medical information retrieval, transforming dense text embeddings into semantically interpretable representations. To do so, our model combines a reconstruction objective with an InfoNCE contrastive loss that directly preserves query-document relevance. We evaluate three embedding models across six medical retrieval datasets, both in-domain and out-of-domain. Trained on a medical corpus, our sparse codes improve in-domain retrieval over the original embeddings, and lose less out-of-domain performance than their dense counterparts, especially when the target corpus is distant from the training one. Importantly, our experiments show that the method yields interpretable features whose semantic content can be inferred from the queries and documents that activate them most strongly. The interpretation of these features sheds light on why a particular document was retrieved, and transfers beyond the training distribution.

### [4] [RACE: Ranking-Uncertainty Aware Capacity-Adaptive Customization for Efficient Sequential Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18244)
*Tianqi Liu, Kaipu Sun, Kairui Fu, Wenkai Wang, Sihao Liu, Hui Zang, Ran Chen, Tao Wang, Shengyu Zhang*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 推荐系统/Recommender System | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: RACE提出面向序列推荐的"排序不确定性感知"实例级自适应压缩框架：用top-K预测分布稳定性构造list-wise排序不确定性指标作为实例复杂度代理，自监督训练轻量预测器从浅层特征提前预测该不确定性，再据此同时做通道级瘦身（超网络式通道敏感性提取）与渐进式序列过滤，实现按需分配算力。

Ranking Relevance: 直接相关于排序/预估系统的高效化与自适应推理。其"预测不确定性→动态算力分配"范式和工业界的动态宽度网络（如AdaEmbed/DynamicFFN、instance-wise early exit、动态序列截断）一脉相承：用列表级排序不确定性替代逐步置信度作为路由信号，更贴近top-K排序目标而非pointwise校准；同时耦合特征（序列）维度与参数（通道）维度两种压缩，对应召回/粗排阶段的时延敏感场景。可迁移点在于：(1) 在粗排/精排中做用户-请求级别的算力预算分配；(2) 序列长度与模型宽度联合自适应；(3) 自监督不确定性预测器可作为级联系统的router。局限是需验证在真实在线流量下不确定性预测器的准确度、动态执行带来的batch效率与GPU并行度损失，以及在多任务（CTR/CVR）目标下不确定性定义的推广。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有序列推荐模型压缩方法普遍采用统一压缩率（one-size-fits-all），忽略了用户意图复杂度的异质性，导致Capacity-Complexity Mismatch：简单样本浪费算力，复杂样本算力不足。因此需要实例级、不确定度自适应的动态压缩。

Method: 1) 从top-K预测的分布稳定性推导list-wise ranking-uncertainty指标，作为实例复杂度的可测代理；2) 用自监督方式训练轻量预测器，从early-layer特征预测该不确定性，避免先跑完整模型；3) 基于该信号在结构维度做hypernetwork式channel sensitivity提取以识别参数冗余，在输入维度做importance-aware progressive sequence filtering以削减行为序列冗余。

Result: 在四个主流数据集上，RACE在准确率、效率、自适应三方面均优于SOTA基线。

Conclusion: 将"排序不确定性"作为实例复杂度代理，可有效指导序列推荐模型的动态结构与输入双重压缩，缓解算力-复杂度失配，具备实时约束下部署的工程价值。

Abstract: Efficient sequential recommendation has emerged as a promising paradigm for providing real-time personalized service. To deploy models under resource-constrained environment, model compression becomes a necessity, yet existing methods typically apply uniform compression ratios, overlooking the heterogeneity in user intent complexity. This "one-size-fits-all" approach leads to a fundamental Capacity-Complexity Mismatch, where computational resources are either wasted on simple instances or insufficient for complex ones. To address this, we propose RACE (Ranking-uncertainty Aware Capacity-Adaptive Customization for Efficient Sequential Recommendation), a framework that dynamically adapts both model structures and input sequences with uncertainty-aware compression ratios. To ensure capacity-adaptive inference, RACE captures the model's intrinsic uncertainty of its predictions and transforms it into a proactive resource allocator. Specifically, we derive a list-wise ranking-uncertainty metric from the distributional stability of top-$K$ predictions, which serves as a measurable proxy for instance complexity. A lightweight predictor is then trained via self-supervision to anticipate this uncertainty from early-layer features. Guided by this signal, RACE performs instance-specific slimming in two dimensions: a hypernetwork-style channel sensitivity extractor identifies parameter redundancy, while importance-aware progressive sequence filtering mitigates data redundancy. Extensive experiments on four popular datasets demonstrate that RACE outperforms state-of-the-art baselines on accuracy, efficiency, and adaptability.

### [5] [MeReader: An Offline, Privacy-Preserving, Progress-Aware AI Assistant for Narrative eBook Reading](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18513)
*Mohammed Efaz, Udo Bub, Itilekha Podder*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 其他/Other | Importance: 低/Low

TL;DR: MeReader 是一个本地离线、隐私保护且"进度感知"的阅读助手：将检索与生成都限制在读者当前阅读位置之前的内容，融合向量相似度、BM25 与摘要嵌入构建混合检索，避免大模型"一次性读全书"导致的剧透；实验显示 2b–4b 中型本地模型可在理解度上追平 5b+ 模型，且进度边界机制能阻止未来内容泄漏而不显著损害检索质量。

Ranking Relevance: 与搜推广排序模型无直接关系：它是一个面向电子书阅读场景的端侧 RAG/对话助手系统，不涉及 LTR、CTR/CVR 预估、多任务学习或重排等排序建模问题。可迁移的思路有两点：(1) 混合检索（dense + BM25 + 摘要粒度索引）的多粒度融合与摘要嵌入召回，与推荐/搜索中的混合召回、多向量表示思路相通；(2) "进度/状态感知的可见性约束"本质上是一种硬性候选集剪枝（类似时间戳或业务状态过滤，可类比为排序前的可行性约束或 hard negative 构造），在工业系统中可用来做因果/可见性一致的特征与样本构造，避免标签泄漏（leakage）。整体而言对工业排序系统的直接落地价值有限，更多是检索系统与端侧部署的参考。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有基于 LLM 的阅读助手通常在线、全文一次性输入，既依赖云端（隐私风险）又会泄露后文造成剧透，破坏叙事体验；同时缺少将"进度感知、隐私保护、电子书阅读 AI 辅助"三者结合的一体化系统。

Conclusion: 在消费级硬件上，通过"上下文受限的混合检索 + 本地中小模型"可以构建隐私友好、无剧透的阅读伴侣，定位于增强而非替代数字阅读体验。

Abstract: This paper introduces MeReader, a privacy-preserving, offline and progress-aware AI assistant designed to function as a contextual companion for narrative reading on consumer hardware. Unlike conventional Large Language Model (LLM)-based systems that process documents/books online in an all-texts-at-once type of way, thereby risking narrative spoilers, MeReader confines both retrieval and generation to the reader's distinct position in the text. The system integrates a hybrid retrieval architecture, combining vector similarity, BM25, and summary embeddings, to ensure high fidelity to the reader's current context. We evaluate the system across classical English books using both quantitative comprehension metrics and qualitative LLM-based independent judging. The results identify a quality-latency frontier, showing that mid-sized local models (2b-4b) can achieve comprehension parity with larger counterparts (5b+) while maintaining acceptable response quality. Importantly, the proposed progress-aware boundary mechanism is shown to prevent future content leakage without compromising retrieval quality and correctness. These findings suggest that a localized, privacy-centric approach can augment, rather than replace, the digital reading experience, offering a good alternative to cloud-dependent services. Therein, there remains a lack of amalgamate systems which combine progress-aware, privacy-preserving, and narrative eBook reading with AI support. It is in this very gap that the development of MeReader came to be; synthesizing said strands into an offline, book-constrained assistant focused on enhancing comprehension without compromising narrative continuity or user privacy.
