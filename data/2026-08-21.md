### [1] [CrossQ: Task-Aligned Cross-Token Conditional Quantization for Late Interaction Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19204)
*Rohit Kumar Salla, Manoj Saravanan, Ramya Manasa Amancherla*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 高/High

TL;DR: CrossQ是一种针对晚期交互检索器（如ColBERT）的新型压缩方案，通过索引期计算但不存储的轻量文档上下文来调节token编码，实现自适应地提升文档中关键"胜出"token的编码保真度。结合对齐排序目标训练，在极低字节/token预算下显著改善MRR@10和nDCG@10，实现64倍原始token存储压缩。

Ranking Relevance: 该论文对排序模型有直接且重要的关联。晚期交互检索器是工业搜索引擎和推荐系统中召回到精排链路的关键组件，其多向量索引的体积直接限制了可部署的候选集规模和系统响应延迟。CrossQ的"压缩目标应对齐排序目标而非重建误差"这一核心洞察具有超越检索的普适意义——它对精排模型（如CTR预估）同样适用：任何压缩/量化模型的训练目标都应围绕最终排序指标（如AUC、MRR、nDCG）而非中间代理损失。此外，CrossQ中"基于文档上下文调节token保真度"的自适应分配思想，可类比到精排特征侧的动态量化与混合精度策略，对工业系统降低成本同时保证排序质量有直接借鉴价值。

Motivation: 晚期交互检索器（如ColBERT）精度高但多向量索引体积巨大，制约了工业部署。传统压缩方法仅最小化token重建误差，但排序效果关键依赖于保留稀疏"胜出"token的得分，这需要保留整个候选得分分布，而不仅仅是重建误差。两者之间存在目标不一致的问题。

Method: CrossQ的核心创新包括：（1）在索引期计算轻量文档上下文，用于调节token编码条件，实现文档内自适应保真度分配（上下文不存储）；（2）训练时使用排序对齐目标，保留候选得分分布并保护困难负样本的间隔（margin），而非简单的重建损失；（3）在2B/token和4B/token等极低比特预算下运作，并支持轻量微调以接近全精度性能。

Result: 在2 B/token下MRR@10较最强严格足迹匹配量化基线提升+0.010，较最强候选匹配系统参考提升+0.012。在BEIR九数据集子集上，4 B/token下平均nDCG@10提升+0.009。4 B/token下实现64倍原始token存储压缩（含元数据约61倍，保守填充对齐约58倍）。8 B/token下轻量微调保留约98%的全精度ColBERT MRR@10。

Conclusion: CrossQ表明，将压缩目标与排序对齐而非最小化重建误差，可以显著改善压缩晚期交互检索器的质量-足迹权衡。这一思路在理论上适用于任何基于晚期交互的检索架构，为内存受限场景下的高质量向量检索提供了实用方案。

Abstract: Late-interaction retrievers like ColBERT achieve high quality but suffer from large multi-vector indices. Standard compression minimizes token reconstruction error, while ranking depends critically on preserving scores of sparse "winner" tokens. We introduce CrossQ, which adaptively improves effective token fidelity within documents by conditioning token codes on lightweight document context computed at indexing time (but not stored). CrossQ is trained with ranking-aligned objectives that preserve candidate score distributions and protect hard-negative margins. At 2 B/token, CrossQ improves MRR@10 by +0.010 over the strongest strictly footprint-matched quantization baseline and by +0.012 over the strongest candidate-matched system reference. On a nine-dataset BEIR subset, CrossQ improves average nDCG@10 by +0.009 at 4 B/token over the strongest candidate-matched system reference. At 4 B/token, CrossQ achieves 64x raw token-storage reduction, approximately 61x including metadata and approximately 58x under conservative padding/alignment accounting. ### [2] [Quantization Beyond Uniform Bit Allocation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19388)
*K. S. Sreeramji, Sabyasachi Basu, Ravishankar Krishnaswamy, Kirankumar Shiragur, Yujia Wang*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 向量检索/Vector Retrieval | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种可变比特分配框架，针对具有Matryoshka结构（MRL）的嵌入向量，在固定的内存预算下，通过将嵌入划分为连续桶并采用贪心策略非均匀分配比特，改进Product Quantization（PQ）和Scalar Quantization（SQ）的压缩质量。在相同压缩比下，PQ和SQ的召回率分别最高提升8%和18%。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有量化方案（如PQ、SQ）大多是嵌入无关的，在维度上均匀分配比特。但现代模型（尤其MRL训练得到的嵌入）具有显著的几何结构——不同维度对信号的重要程度不同。在低比特场景下，均匀分配会严重浪费存储资源，导致量化误差过大，召回率受损。本文旨在提出一种结构感知的量化方案，在内存预算固定的前提下提升检索质量。

Method: 提出可变比特分配框架：1）将嵌入向量划分为多个连续桶（contiguous buckets）；2）基于贪心策略（greedy allocation）在不同桶间非均匀分配比特数，优先将更多比特分配给信息量更高（对量化误差影响更大）的桶；3）将该框架分别实例化到Product Quantization（PQ）和Scalar Quantization（SQ）两种主流量化方法中。利用MRL嵌入的特性——低维子空间已包含大量信息——设计贪心分配目标函数，在每轮迭代中将额外比特分配给能最大程度降低量化误差的桶。

Result: 在具有Matryoshka属性（MRL）的嵌入上进行了系列实验：1）非均匀分配在相同存储预算下一致优于均匀分配基线；2）低比特场景下增益最大，因为此时均匀分配对MRL嵌入效率极低；3）在相同压缩率下，可变分配使PQ召回率最多提升8%，SQ召回率最多提升18%。

Conclusion: 提出了一种新的结构感知压缩与索引方向：量化方案不应再一味地按维度均匀分配比特，而应利用嵌入本身的几何结构（如MRL的层次化信息分布）来优化资源分配。这一思路可以推广到大规模检索系统中，为embedding压缩、近似最近邻搜索和向量索引提供更高效的方案。

Abstract: Quantization is a fundamental technique to handle the growing sizes of embeddings generated by modern models. Existing quantization schemes are largely embedding agnostic and allocate bits uniformly across dimensions. However, recent models produce embeddings with significant geometric structure. In this work, we investigate whether a variable bit allocation scheme can improve quantization quality under a fixed memory budget. We propose a simple variable bit allocation framework that partitions an embedding into contiguous buckets and allocates storage non-uniformly across them. Using a greedy allocation strategy, we instantiate this framework for both Product Quantization (PQ) and Scalar Quantization (SQ). We perform a series of experiments on embeddings known to have the Matryoshka property (MRL), and consistently observe that non-uniform allocations outperform uniform baselines at identical storage budgets. The largest improvements occur in the low-bit regime, where uniform allocation is particularly inefficient for MRL embeddings. At the same compression rates, variable allocation improves recall by up to 8\% for PQ and up to 18\% for SQ. Our results suggest a new direction for structure-aware compression and indexing techniques for large-scale retrieval systems.

Motivation: 现有 BrowseComp-Plus 基准虽然将 agentic search 与检索器解耦，但其语料仅含约10万文档，且证据和干扰项都是针对查询精选的，无法真实反映大规模开放网络环境下的检索难度。需要一个能将基准"投影"到真实规模、未针对基准定制的大型语料上的方法，从而分离并评估 agent 推理与检索能力的真实挑战。

Method: 提出一个数据感知的投影 pipeline：(1) 将每个问题分解为原子推理步骤（atomic reasoning hops）；(2) 在新的目标语料（ClimbMix）中为每个 hop 定位支持证据；(3) 通过三重验证——自动验证、独立 agent 验证、人工审核——确保每个 hop 在新语料中均有充分支持，只有全部通过才保留问题。该 pipeline 是数据集无关的，适用于任何可分解为可验证事实的基准。

Result: 在830道 BrowseComp-Plus 测试题上，pipeline 产出57道完全有据可依的问题并带有问题级相关性判断。最强的 agent 在投影后的基准上答案准确率下降5个点，证据召回率从84.3%骤降至21.4%，同时搜索调用量增加63%，表明投影将难度从推理转移到了检索。

Conclusion: 投影后的基准成功将 agentic search 评估的重心转移至真实规模的检索挑战，提供了一种可复现、数据集无关的方法论来构建更贴近实际的大规模检索评估基准。该 pipeline、基准和分析均已开源发布。

Abstract: The BrowseComp-Plus benchmark disentangled the evaluation of agentic search by replacing opaque web search with a fixed corpus, so that an agent's role can be separated from the retriever's. That corpus, however, holds only about 100K documents and was assembled from the supporting documents of the benchmark's own queries plus mined hard negatives, so the evidence and the distractors were both selected per query. We introduce $\text{BrowseComp-Plus}_{\text{CM}}$, which keeps the BrowseComp-Plus questions but relocates their evidence to ClimbMix, a 400B-token, 553M-document mixture of web text released by NVIDIA for pre-training language models and built without reference to any benchmark. Our main contribution is the projection pipeline that makes this possible: it decomposes each question into atomic reasoning hops and grounds every hop in the new corpus, retaining a question only when automatic verification, an independent agent, and human review all confirm that every hop is supported. The pipeline is dataset-agnostic and applies to any benchmark whose questions decompose into verifiable facts. Applied to the 830 BrowseComp-Plus test questions, our pipeline yields 57 fully grounded questions with question-level relevance judgments. Projection shifts the difficulty onto retrieval, as the strongest agent we evaluate loses five points of answer accuracy but sees its evidence recall fall from 84.3% to 21.4% while issuing 63% more search calls. As the first of a series of projections, we release the pipeline, the benchmark, and our analyses at https://github.com/castorini/cmass.
