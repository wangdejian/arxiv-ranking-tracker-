### [1] [LIFT: A Lifecycle-aware Interaction Factorization Transformer for Unified Retrieval and Ranking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10556)
*Keji Miao, Enhao Cheng, Yan Li, Qun Li, Qi Zhang, Jie Yuan, Xiaoyong Li*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 推荐系统/Recommender System | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: LIFT将用户每次交互拆解为Request/Item/Context/Action四类有序状态并建模为因果序列，召回读Request状态、排序读Context状态，实现阶段共享历史建模又保留阶段特有信息；在ML-20M与Taobao上Joint Score分别超最强基线4.9%和3.6%。

Ranking Relevance: 直接相关：论文针对级联推荐中召回与排序的历史建模一致性问题，属于排序模型与召回模型的联合训练范畴。其"同一序列、不同阶段读不同状态"的思路可迁移到搜推广的pre-ranking/ranking一致性、样本与特征时序对齐等工程问题，对统一多阶段建模（如generative recommendation、one-model范式）有参考价值。但实验限于公开数据集，缺少在线A/B与延迟/吞吐指标，对工业排序系统的即时效用有限。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 级联推荐系统中召回与排序复用同一份用户历史，但两阶段在交互的不同时点可获取的信息其实不同：召回发生在用户请求发生时，尚无候选物品与后续行为上下文；排序则能看到本次交互的Context与候选物品。直接用同一历史表示会引入信息错配（信息泄漏或阶段信息缺失），因此需要一种能按交互生命周期区分阶段可见信息的统一序列表征。

Method: 提出Lifecycle-aware Interaction Factorization Transformer（LIFT）：把每次交互分解为有序的Request→Item→Context→Action状态，作为因果序列建模；通过Role-Conditioned Attention（按角色/阶段条件化注意力）在共享序列建模的同时为召回与排序提供不同读出口——召回读Request状态，排序读Context状态；并引入轻量Pre-LN Bias以低成本编码角色先验。

Conclusion: LIFT表明：召回与排序不必共用同一份历史快照，而应共享带生命周期角色标注的因果序列，并各自读取所处阶段可见的状态。该"阶段条件化读出口"的设计为级联系统提供了统一的联合建模范式，且实现开销可控（轻量Pre-LN Bias），具备向工业级两阶段系统迁移的潜力。

Abstract: Cascaded recommender systems use the same user history for retrieval and ranking, while the two stages have access to different information at different points of an interaction. We propose the Lifecycle-aware Interaction Factorization Transformer (LIFT), which decomposes each interaction into ordered Request, Item, Context, and Action states and models them as a causal sequence. Retrieval reads the Request state, while ranking reads the Context state, allowing both tasks to share history modeling while preserving stage-specific information. LIFT instantiates this representation with Role-Conditioned Attention and a lightweight Pre-LN Bias. On ML-20M and Taobao, LIFT achieves the highest Joint Score among the evaluated joint models, improving over the strongest baselines by 4.9% and 3.6%, respectively. Loss-weight sweeps show favorable retrieval--ranking trade-offs, while ablations and scaling analyses further examine lifecycle sequence construction, model components, and capacity settings.

Motivation: 传统CQR在对话历史基础上单步改写，改写结果在获得任何语料证据之前就已固定，导致指代消解与用词无法被检索结果纠正，构成"盲改写"瓶颈。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: POI 局部搜索中，查询意图与词法语义、地理邻近性、数值质量信号（评分、评论数）之间存在条件化权衡：近的 POI 可能只部分满足意图，远的 POI 可能语义和质量更强。现有 LTR（如 XGBoost）以标量加权和为主，难以刻画语义-数值的非线性交互；纯 LLM 重排虽语义强但零样本效果差且难以满足实时服务约束。因此需要一个既显式建模异构信号交互、又满足时延预算的重排方案。

A close POI may only partially satisfy the query intent, while a farther one may offer stronger semantic and quality evidence. We present H2CE, a Heterogeneous Two-stage Cross-Encoder for latency-bounded POI reranking. H2CE represents numerical attributes in two complementary ways: bucketized natural-language descriptors are inserted into the cross-encoder input to support semantic--numeric attention, while exact scalar values are processed by dedicated MLPs to preserve magnitude information. The resulting semantic and numerical embeddings are fused through latent-space aggregation, enabling nonlinear interactions beyond scalar weighted sums. H2CE then applies a two-stage architecture: Stage 1 scores all candidates pointwise for scalable filtering, and Stage 2 performs head-to-head pairwise comparison among the top-$K$ candidates with Copeland aggregation, making fine-grained relative tradeoffs explicit while reducing pairwise cost from O(N^2) to O(N+K(K-1)). The pairwise stage adds +1.98% NDCG@5 over the pointwise model alone. Ablations confirm the value of numerical features, latent aggregation, top-K pairwise reranking, and aligned training.

  <summary>Details</summary>

Building on SEISMIC, we revisit both levels of its design: the inverted index used to select candidates and the forward index used to score them. For the inverted index, we replace costly per-block summaries with medoids, namely existing documents elected as block representatives, collapsing the per-block metadata from a sparse vector to a single document identifier. For the forward index, we compress both components and values. We reorder the vocabulary to place co-occurring components closer together and encode the resulting $Δ$-gaps with DOTPACKING8, a SIMD-friendly bit-packing scheme that fuses decompression with dot-product evaluation; values are quantized with compact per-component 4-bit codebooks fitted to each component's distribution. We further introduce JUMPDOT, a blocked dot-product kernel tailored for queries that contain only a few non-zero entries. Our forward-index compression is independent of SEISMIC and can be plugged into any system relying on forward-index-based scoring, as we demonstrate by integrating it into KANNOLO. A comprehensive evaluation on MS MARCO with three state-of-the-art learned sparse encoders shows that our solutions markedly improve the speed-space trade-off of learned sparse retrieval: at equal accuracy, our indexes answer queries up to 5.3x faster than the best competitor while using about 3x less memory, and in the most memory-constrained regime, they remain up to 1.9x faster while using up to 3.9x less memory.
