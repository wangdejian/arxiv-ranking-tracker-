### [2] [GRaSP: Latency-Aware Optimization for Geo-Distributed Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26938)
*Hardik Ruparel, Raj Kumar Pandey, Pramod Kumar*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 将RAG检索的重排序层建模为“地理感知选择问题”(GRaSP)，在基数预算与分区上限约束下，用延迟衰减相关度并可选加入跨区出网成本惩罚，无需重训练；证明基础形式是分区拟阵上的线性目标、可O(N log N)贪心最优求解，实测显著降低P90取回延迟且保持大部分召回。

Motivation: RAG increasingly runs across geo-distributed cloud/edge, where WAN latency, cross-region egress cost and data-residency constraints conflict with retrieval quality. Conventional retrieval ranks purely by semantic similarity, ignoring content location, thus inflating tail latency and cost.

Method: Formalize GRaSP: a drop-in re-scoring layer that scores candidates by relevance decayed by latency plus optional cost penalty, subject to a cardinality budget and per-region caps, without retraining. Abstract: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is increasingly served across geo-distributed cloud and edge infrastructures, where wide-area latency, cross-region egress cost, and data-residency rules compete with retrieval quality. Conventional pipelines rank by semantic similarity alone and ignore where content lives, inflating tail latency and cost. ### [3] [UIBK-Embedding: Effective Text Retrieval by Unifying Dense and Late-Interaction Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27195)
*Abdelrahman Abdallah, Mohammed Ali, Adam Jatowt*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 中/Medium

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 稠密双塔（bi-encoder）与late-interaction检索器通常被视为互斥方案，但二者失效的query分布不同：单向量刻画"文本讲什么"（语义/主题），token级匹配捕捉精确实体与短语。工业上同时用两者需要两套模型、两套索引再做分数融合，成本与复杂度高。作者希望用单模型单索引、单一打分函数同时获得两种能力。

Conclusion: 通过"稠密向量嵌入token向量列表 + tag隔离 + 统一MaxSim"的设计，把双塔与ColBERT式late-interaction在表示层和打分层真正统一，单模型单索引即可兼顾语义召回与精确词面匹配，小规模模型（179M）在BEIR上逼近大模型水平。对工业召回侧"双路召回+分数融合"架构提供了低成本替代方案。

Abstract: Dense bi-encoders and late-interaction retrievers are usually treated as alternatives, yet they fail on different queries: one vector captures what a text is about, token-level matching finds its exact entities and phrases. Using both today means two models, two indexes and a score-fusion step. We present UIBK-Embedding-v1, a 179M-parameter English encoder that produces both representations in one pass and scores them with one unmodified MaxSim. The dense embedding is written into the list of token vectors as three extra vectors, and a one-dimensional tag keeps the two kinds of vectors from matching each other, so that plain MaxSim equals the token-level MaxSim plus a weighted dense cosine. The model therefore runs in a standard late-interaction index without changes; we test it with PLAID. A shared 18-layer trunk with a separate 4-layer top per head, and a cross-encoder distilled into the fused score, turn the two heads into one retriever. On BEIR, UIBK-Embedding-v1 reaches 59.79 nDCG@10. This is 2.57 points above LateOn, the strongest model of its size, and 2.10 points above it when both models read documents of the same length; UIBK-Embedding-v1 is ahead on all 15 datasets. On a decontaminated version of BEIR it stays ahead on average (61.55 against 60.36) and on 8 of 12 datasets. Among the 156 models with complete BEIR results in the MTEB results repository, only models with at least 1.5B parameters score higher. Code, model weights, and a technical blog post are available at https://github.com/DataScienceUIBK/uibk-embedding , https://huggingface.co/DataScience-UIBK/uibk-embedding-v1 , and https://huggingface.co/blog/abdoelsayed/uibk-embedding-v1 .
