### [1] [Learning the Platform, Not the Learner: Sequencing Shortcuts in the Offline Evaluation of Educational Recommender Systems and LLM Rankers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33240)

Motivation: 教育推荐/学习路径推荐的离线评测通常以"预测日志中的下一步活动"作为代理任务，但这些下一步往往由平台（教师布置、导师选题、引擎出题）而非学习者自主决定。这导致日志中的"下一个活动"高度可被平台规则与群体行为模式解释，从而让简单的结构性基线（如群体转移频率）与个性化模型难以区分，甚至出现"模仿平台"优于"满足学习者需求"的错误排序。作者希望量化这一评测偏差，并给出更贴近真实效用的评测协议。

Weighting test steps by how unlikely the structure makes them restores much of the link with usefulness and, on the real data, reveals a significant personalisation signal on seven of the eight datasets. We propose a syllabus-aware evaluation checklist and release a Python package that reports the lift of any recommender over the structure.
