### [1] [TRACES: A Benchmark for Epistemic Reliability in Scientific Reasoning by LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11415)
*Valentin Rodionov, Shamil Assylbekov*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 其他/Other | Importance: 低/Low

TL;DR: 本文提出了一套探针语料库（42篇被撤回、欺诈和伪科学论文）和配套方法论，用于直接衡量大语言模型区分可靠与不可靠科学文献的能力。通过五种声明类型的探针测试发现，30个模型中所有模型在超过71%的探针上失败，22/30个模型失败率超过90%，说明模型的拒绝行为主要依赖特定主题而非真正的科学判断能力，亟需建立护栏基础设施。

Ranking Relevance: 该论文的核心研究对象是LLM在科学工作流中的可靠性评估，并不直接涉及搜索/推荐/广告领域。但存在间接关联：①LLM在搜索和推荐系统中正被用作Agent或生成式排序引擎，其"信任不可靠来源"的失败模式可能影响检索结果的可靠性；②该文的探针方法和评估框架（衡量模型是否基于内容本身而非表面信号做判断）可类比于排序模型中的鲁棒性评估，特别是对抗性样本与分布外泛化的评估思路；③"主题导向的拒绝行为而非真正的认知能力"这一发现，对理解和改进生成式推荐系统的安全对齐具有参考价值。总体而言，作为排序模型研究方向的相关性较弱。

Motivation: 大语言模型正被用作科学工作流中的智能代理，但在缺乏下游验证器的领域，模型需要具备区分可靠与不可靠科学文献的能力，而这一能力尚未被直接测量。现有基准只评估有已知答案的事实性问题，无法覆盖本次针对的失败模式——模型在面对被撤回、欺诈或伪科学论文的框架时是否能识别其不可靠性。

Conclusion: 模型当前的"拒绝"行为是主题导向的安全机制，而非真正的科学可靠性与不可靠性辨别能力。在无验证器的科学工作流中部署LLM代理存在显著风险，亟需构建专门的护栏基础设施来应对这一问题。

Abstract: Large language models are being proposed as agents in scientific workflows, in domains where no downstream verifier exists. Such deployment assumes the model can distinguish reliable scientific literature from unreliable literature, a capability that has not yet been directly measured. Existing benchmarks evaluate factuality on questions with known answers; the failure mode we target here is different. We introduce a probe corpus of 42 retracted, fraudulent, and pseudoscientific papers, paired with a methodology for eliciting and scoring single-shot model engagement with each paper's framing. Each probe pairs a preamble extracted near-verbatim from the target paper with a scientifically plausible study-design request. The probes span five claim types: fabricated observation, pseudophysical mechanism, magical premise, legitimization bridge, and cargo-cult experiment. Two complementary scores measure whether a model rejects the flawed premise outright (IFR-a) and whether it recognizes the unreliability while still engaging (IFR-i). Every evaluated model fails more than 71% of agentic probes, and 22 of 30 models fail more than 90% of the time. Rejections are concentrated on a small number of high-notoriety topics and specific probes, and disappear under matched-structure controls. These results are consistent with topic-keyed safety behavior rather than robust epistemic competence, and indicate an urgent need for guardrail infrastructure for scientific deployment of language models.

### [2] [From Overlooked to Explored: Recovering Item Relations via Mixture of Perspectives for Sequential Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11846)
*Junyoung Kim, Wonbin Kweon, Woojoo Kim, Jaehyung Lim, Dongha Kim, Hwanjo Yu*

Motivation: 序列推荐的核心挑战在于从用户交互序列中捕获偏好，而该偏好内嵌于物品间关系之中。自注意力虽能计算逐对物品交互，但实证分析发现其存在系统性相似性偏差：点积注意力分数不成比例地偏向相似物品，忽略携带重要偏好信号的异质关系，从而限制了推荐性能。这是对Transformer-based SR模型固有缺陷的直接诊断与修复。

Method: PRISM模块包含K个Perspective Lenses，从不同视角校准注意力分数。每个Lens结合两个子视图：(1) Affinity View——细化同质（相似）关系，精炼正向偏好信号；(2) Contrast View——暴露被相似性偏差压制的异质（对比）关系，捕捉互补性偏好。通过多视角透镜的组合，模型能够覆盖用户偏好的完整谱系，弥补标准点积注意力仅关注相似性的局限。

Result: 在7个真实世界基准数据集上的大量实验表明，PRISM一致性地优于现有最强基线（SOTA）方法。同时论文提供了对多类型Transformer-based SR模型相似性偏差的实证分析作为支撑。

Conclusion: PRISM通过多视角校准自注意力，有效解决了Transformer序列推荐中的相似性偏差问题，证明了异质关系对捕获用户完整偏好的关键价值。该模块设计轻量、即插即用，可集成到现有Transformer-based SR框架中。

Abstract: Capturing user preference from a user's interaction sequence is the central challenge of Sequential Recommendation (SR). This preference intuitively emerges from inter-item relations: each item transition reflects a preference embedded in the relations between items, making the faithful capture of these relations essential for accurate recommendation. For this reason, self-attention is dominant in sequential recommendation for its ability to compute pairwise item interactions, yet our empirical analysis reveals that it consistently suffers from similarity bias across various types of transformer-based SR models: dot-product attention scores disproportionately favor similar items, systematically overlooking heterogeneous relations with meaningful preference signals and directly limiting recommendation performance. To address this, we propose PRISM (Perspective-based Relational Insight Synthesis Module), a module that re-examines item relations from multiple perspectives. PRISM employs K Perspective Lenses to calibrate attention from distinct viewpoints, combining an Affinity View that refines homogeneous relations and a Contrast View that exposes heterogeneous ones suppressed by similarity bias, enabling the model to capture the full spectrum of user preferences. Extensive experiments on seven real-world benchmarks demonstrate that PRISM consistently outperforms state-of-the-art baselines. Our code is available at https://github.com/327aem/PRISM/.
