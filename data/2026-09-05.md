### [1] [CHSR-RRF: A curriculum-gated hybrid retrieval framework with reciprocal rank fusion and leakage-aware benchmarking for educational RAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02913)
*Terence Ateya, Zavier Ndum Ndum, Jicheng Fu, Kelly Tendongkeng*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 其他/Other（检索增强生成RAG与教育领域的混合检索） | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 本文提出CHSR-RRF，一种面向教育领域RAG的课程约束混合检索框架，在检索前施加元数据约束（学科、年级、考试情境），再结合稀疏/稠密检索与倒数排名融合及确定性重排。同时提出CERB基准数据集（126个案例），包含层级感知相关性标签和"课程泄漏"标注。结果表明预检索门控可将泄漏降低4.6倍且保持排序召回率。

Motivation: 标准RAG检索器仅优化主题相关性，未强制课程有效性（curriculum validity）。在教育场景中，一篇相关文本可能来自错误学科、年级或考试情境，出现"课程泄漏"（curriculum leakage）失败模式。论文旨在解决这一问题。

Method: 1) CHSR-RRF框架：检索前进行元数据约束门控（curriculum-gated pre-retrieval filtering），再结合BM25稀疏检索与稠密向量检索，使用倒数排名融合（RRF）及确定性重排。2) CERB基准：126个案例，含层级感知相关性标签和显式泄漏标注。3) 对比实验：预检索门控 vs 检索后约束（post-retrieval constraint）。

We also introduce CERB, a 126-case benchmark for curriculum-constrained retrieval with hierarchy-aware relevance labels and explicit leakage annotations. On a 61-case pilot, pre-retrieval gating reduces leakage by 4.6x ($p<0.001$) while preserving ranked recall, whereas applying the same constraints after retrieval collapses recall and exact-scope success to zero ($p=0.039$). A full-benchmark lower-bound analysis further shows that many remaining failures arise from corpus and metadata gaps rather than retrieval design alone. These results show that retrieval in structured educational domains should be treated as constrained selection, with validity enforced when the candidate pool is formed rather than after ranking.
