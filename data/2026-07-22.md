### [1] [Topology-Aware Tokenization for Generative Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18600)
*Yaokun Liu, Yifan Liu, Zhenrui Yue, Gyuseok Lee, Zelin Li, Ruichen Yao, Dong Wang*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 排序模型/Ranking | Importance: 高/High

TL;DR: 针对生成式推荐中物品分词导致的拓扑扭曲问题，提出TopoTok框架，通过多级蒸馏（组间、组内、物品间）在量化层次中保留物品关系结构，显著提升推荐准确率。

Ranking Relevance: 直接相关。生成式推荐本质上是将推荐建模为序列生成，其最后一阶段的物品生成（预测下一个token）本质是一个排序问题——模型需要对候选物品的token进行概率排序。TopoTok通过改善物品tokenization的拓扑保真度，直接提升了生成阶段的序质量（Recall@5），对工业场景中的生成式排序模型有重要参考价值。此外，多级蒸馏的思想也可迁移到传统排序模型的特征嵌入学习中。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有生成式推荐将物品序列建模为自回归生成任务，但在物品分词（tokenization）阶段，预训练语义嵌入空间的邻接关系在量化后被严重破坏，导致模型对物品相似性的感知错误，成为推荐精度的瓶颈。

Method: 提出TopoTok框架，包含三级蒸馏方案：1) 组间蒸馏（Inter-Group Distillation）捕获全局聚类关系；2) 组内蒸馏（Intra-Group Distillation）细化语义簇内局部结构；3) 物品间蒸馏（Inter-Item Distillation）在个体粒度强制执行细粒度对齐。

Result: 在三个基准数据集上，TopoTok一致优于SOTA分词器，Recall@5提升高达9.42%。

Conclusion: 拓扑感知的分词策略能有效缓解生成式推荐中的拓扑扭曲问题，是提升生成式推荐精度的关键方向。

Abstract: Generative recommendation reformulates sequential recommendation as an autoregressive generation task, yet a critical issue in this paradigm remains overlooked: topology distortion in item tokenization. In particular, we observe that the intrinsic adjacency relationships of items in the pretrained semantic embedding space are significantly disrupted after quantization. This topology distortion misleads the model's perception of item similarity, ultimately bottlenecking the accuracy of generative recommendations. To address this issue, we propose Topology-Aware Tokenization (TopoTok), an item tokenization framework that preserves item relational structure throughout the quantization hierarchy. Different from the prior monolithic supervision in tokenization, TopoTok introduces a multi-level distillation scheme to progressively recover the topology from coarse to fine granularity: 1) Inter-Group Distillation to capture global cluster-wise relations; 2) Intra-Group Distillation to refine local structures within semantic clusters; and 3) Inter-Item Distillation to enforce fine-grained alignment at the individual item level. Extensive experiments on three benchmark datasets demonstrate that TopoTok effectively alleviates topology distortion and consistently outperforms state-of-the-art tokenizers, achieving significant performance gains of up to 9.42% in Recall@5.

### [2] [AutoIndex: Learning Representation Programs for Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18603)
*Sam O'Nuallain, Nithya Rajkumar, Ramya Narayanasamy, Hanna Jiang, Shreyas Chaudhari, Andrew Drozdov*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 中/Medium

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统检索系统中，文档表示通常被视为索引前的固定预处理步骤（如分词、停用词过滤等），但这种静态处理方式无法针对不同检索任务自适应优化，导致检索质量受限。作者旨在将文档表示本身作为显式优化目标，而非固定的预处理选择。

Method: AutoIndex采用验证导向的程序搜索（validation-guided program search）框架：每轮迭代中，智能体诊断当前程序（由一系列可执行转换操作组成）的失败模式，并综合成候选更新，仅保留能提升检索质量的更新。这些程序可以切片、丰富、归一化、重加权或重组文档。检索器固定为BM25，不调优检索器或重排序器。

Abstract: We present AutoIndex, a framework for learning representation programs: executable transformations that map raw documents into the representations exposed to a retrieval system. We evaluate AutoIndex on CRUMB, a benchmark of heterogeneous retrieval tasks, with BM25 held fixed across all experiments. The learned programs improve recall over a static full-document BM25 baseline on all 8 tasks, with average gains of +8.4% in Recall@100 and +8.3% in nDCG@10, and largest gains of +30.5% in Recall@100 and +43.6% in nDCG@10. These results suggest that document representation should not be treated as a fixed preprocessing choice made before retrieval begins, but as an explicit optimization target. Code to reproduce our results is available at https://github.com/auto-index/autoindex.
