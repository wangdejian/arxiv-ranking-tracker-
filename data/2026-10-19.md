### [1] [NoveltyScope: A Multi-Agent Research Assistant for Evidence-Grounded Novelty Assessment of Scientific Papers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22703)

### [2] [Reasoning Reranking with Semantic IDs as the Sole Item Representation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22797)
*Zhenwei Tang, Jintao Liu, Di Bai, Xidong Wu, Xinwu Cheng*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 重排模型/Reranking | Importance: 高

TL;DR: 论文构建 ReRankID 测试床，检验生成式推荐模型仅凭 Semantic ID（SID）对候选列表做推理重排的能力；发现未后训练的模型会固定复制输入顺序，而监督微调、拒绝采样自训练、on-policy 蒸馏、基于验证器的 RL 均可激发重排行为，其中 8B 模型在分级排序奖励 RL 下达到最佳 SID-only 效果，匹配文本提示长约 20 倍的强 LLM。

Ranking Relevance: 直接面向重排环节：研究 LLM-based reasoning reranker 在 SID 表示下的可行性与后训练配方，对生成式推荐/向量化 ID 体系下的重排落地有直接指导意义。工业价值在于：SID 提示比物品文本短约 20 倍，可显著降低预填与上下文成本，并在长历史（>50k token）场景保持增益，适合大规模候选 slate 重排；分级排序奖励 RL 与 on-policy 蒸馏是可复用的后训练路径。局限是 rationale 忠实度不足，需关注可解释性与描述准确性对业务审校的影响。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有推理式重排器依赖物品元数据文本构建 rationale，而生成式推荐已普遍用紧凑 SID 表示物品。用 SID-only 重排有两大吸引力：提示长度远短于匹配的文本；SID 的 token embedding 部分由用户-物品交互序列学习，可携带文本缺失的行为信号。但 SID 训练目标是 next-item generation，其是否支持对给定候选列表做排序尚未验证。

Result: 未后训练的模型常复制输入顺序而非重排，可读元数据也无法修复该失败；四种后训练方法均能激发重排行为。最佳模型（8B + 分级排序奖励 RL）取得最优 SID-only 排序效果，匹配提示长约 20 倍的强读文本 LLM；在零历史条件下仍优于随机（caption 参考模型不能），长历史下（文本提示超 50k token、caption 参考不再提升）仍保持增益。rationale 审计显示该模型权衡论证优于参考但物品描述准确性更差。

That training targets next-item generation, and whether it supports ranking a supplied slate from SIDs alone is untested. On this testbed, such models often copy the input order instead of reranking, and readable metadata does not repair the failure. The strongest post-trained model, the 8B model trained with a graded ranking reward, gives the best SID-only ranking and matches strong text-reading LLMs whose prompts are about 20x longer. It ranks above random with no history at all, where a caption-reading reference does not, and it keeps its gains at long histories, where text prompts pass 50k tokens and the caption-reading reference stops improving. Motivation: 生成式引擎(如AI搜索/推荐式助手)依赖可被内容提供方通过GEO编辑的外部信源，进而影响推荐结果。用户选择依赖哪个AI系统，需要知道该系统在GEO攻击/优化下的鲁棒性与效果。但现有基准对GEO策略空间与编辑约束的定义各不相同，使得不同基准间的指标增益不可比（算法优劣与编辑自由度混同），覆盖度也难以横向比较。

Method: 提出统一评测标准的倡议，并构造一个元分类法(meta-taxonomy)，按三个维度刻画GEO策略：编辑位置(where)、编辑方式(how)、所针对的信号(what signals)；将代表性文献中的策略映射进该可检索空间，并设计受控对比协议，要求显式报告覆盖度(coverage reporting)。

Result: 论文为立场/框架型工作，未给出定量实验结果；输出为一个可检索的GEO策略空间映射、一套受控比较协议与覆盖度报告规范，可作为公共leaderboard的组织基础。

Conclusion: GEO评测亟需统一标准与受控比较；通过"位置-方式-信号"元分类法和覆盖度报告，可以把优化算法能力与编辑自由度解耦，支撑跨系统可比、可信的排行榜，从而帮助用户做出"最后的选择"。

Abstract: Humanity's Last Choice describes the idea that the last choice people need to make is which AI system they entrust with the decisions that follow. These systems rely on external sources that content providers can edit through generative engine optimization (GEO) to increase source visibility and influence recommendations. Choosing among these systems therefore requires comparable evidence of how well they carry out user requests when exposed to GEO. Benchmarks have made progress in standardizing tasks, models, and metrics, but they often define their own spaces of GEO strategies and use different editing constraints. These differences can conflate gains from better optimization algorithms with those from greater freedom to edit sources, while making evaluation coverage difficult to compare across benchmarks. We therefore call for a common evaluation standard and propose a meta-taxonomy that describes where sources are edited, how they are edited, and what signals those edits target. Drawing on representative literature, we map existing strategies into a searchable space and outline a protocol for controlled comparisons with explicit coverage reporting. This provides a foundation for common leaderboards that can inform which systems people choose to rely on.
