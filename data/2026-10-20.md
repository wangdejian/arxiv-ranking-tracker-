### [1] [When Does Evidence-Graph Structure Predict Answer Correctness? Label-Free Abstention for Structured Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24030)
*Anjan Goswami*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 搜索引擎/Search Engine | Importance: 低

TL;DR: 提出一种免训练的"弃答（abstention）"方法：在证据图上对已选证据做边界切割（boundary cut），用已选/未选候选间的加权割作为可信度信号，无需正确性标签或置信度模型训练。在 MemoryAgentBench FactConsolidation 多跳任务上，AUC-PC 从最优 score baseline 的 0.736 提升到 0.785（+0.049），原生评测下差距 +0.108；零割子集覆盖 71/400 查询且观测精度 100%。但监督 GBDT 选择器在同栈下可达 0.814，且外部基准收益依赖图构造与边语义。

Ranking Relevance: 面向多跳检索/Agentic RAG 的"后检索弃答"，属于检索管线的置信度校准与过滤环节，与工业搜广推主排序链路的直接关联较弱。可借鉴点：①以图割/结构闭合作为无标签置信特征，可迁入生成式检索、答案召回的可信度打分或 rerank 后的低质候选裁剪；②其"零割高精度子集"思路类似高精度阈值分层（precision-coverage 曲线），可用于分层曝光策略与护栏；③与 GBDT 选择器的对比提示实际落地应以结构信号为特征、用有监督重排融合。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 检索系统只返回分数，高相似度分数并不等价于答案可信；缺乏正确性标签时难以训练置信度模型，因此希望利用已有证据图结构，以零训练、可审计的方式实现检索后弃答，避免额外模型推理开销。

Method: training-free：对选中证据子集与时序/未选候选集合之间的加权割（weighted cut）作为置信度信号（割越小越可信）；将未解析查询统一排在末位以做公平比较；分析零割子集与结构闭包（structural closure）的对应关系，并做控制实验拆分"解析状态"与连续边权、关系类型的贡献。

Result: MAB FactConsolidation 多跳：AUC-PC 0.736→0.785（+0.049, 95% CI [+0.008,+0.092]），原生评测 +0.108；零割子集 71/400 查询、100% 观测精度；剩余区分度主要来自 resolution status，连续边权收益有限；外部基准上收益随图构造与边语义而变，仅"有类型结构"不足以保证有效置信度；同检索栈下监督 GBDT 0.814 优于 0.785。

Conclusion: 证据图上的边界割可作为轻量、可审计、免训练的后处理弃答信号，但并非普适；其效果受图构造质量与解析状态主导，与有监督选择器仍有差距，适合作为低成本兜底/过滤层而非替代学习式置信度模型。

Abstract: Retrieval systems return scored answers, but a high score does not show that an answer should be trusted. The proposed training-free abstention method applies the boundary cut to the selected evidence in an evidence graph. The signal is the weighted cut between selected and unselected candidates and requires no correctness labels or confidence-model training. On MemoryAgentBench (MAB) FactConsolidation multi-hop, with unresolved queries ranked last for every selector, it raises area under the precision-coverage curve from 0.736 for the best score baseline to 0.785 (+0.049, 95% CI [+0.008, +0.092]); under the native evaluation, where score baselines keep their unresolved values, the gap is +0.108. A zero-cut subset contains 71 of 400 queries at 100% observed precision. In this pipeline, the subset corresponds to structural closure; controlled analyses attribute much of the remaining separation to resolution status, with little benefit from continuous edge weights. Across external benchmarks, usefulness varies with graph construction, edge semantics, and relation; typed structure alone does not ensure useful confidence. A supervised gradient-boosted selector achieves 0.814 versus the boundary cut's 0.785 when the retrieval stack matches. These findings provide empirical guidance for using available evidence graphs for lightweight, auditable post-retrieval abstention without additional model inference.

### [2] [Location, Location, Generation: Where You Ask Matters for Generative Engine Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24081)
*Mahe Chen, Nick Koudas*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 搜索引擎/Search Engine | Importance: 中

TL;DR: 对 ChatGPT 与 Gemini 做受控审计（31,200 条回答），发现两引擎取用地理位置的通道不同：ChatGPT 遵循应用声明的城市级位置并写入自身查询，搜索后端即使查询未含地名也会本地化；Gemini 忽略声明位置而跟随网络所在国家。引用源与推荐商家的变化随位置而变。

Ranking Relevance: 与经典排序模型关系间接，但属于生成式检索（RAG 式搜索）的上层评测问题：其一，揭示位置/地理是检索阶段的一等特征，说明工业检索与排序链路中地理位置信号（contextual geo feature）会经由查询改写与后端索引分区两层影响最终结果，对本地搜索、LBS 广告的召回索引切分与位置特征归一化有参考意义；其二，多位置、重复采样的评测设计对排序系统的鲁棒性与 A/B 实验噪声估计（run-to-run variance）有方法论借鉴；其三，对 GEO/内容方而言，来源可见度随位置分层，意味着排序中的来源多样性、公平性与归因评估需要按地理位置切片。但本文不涉及具体排序模型结构或打分函数，对模型层面的创新推动有限。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 生成式引擎优化（GEO）实践通常从单一地点监测品牌与来源可见度，但未验证"用户位置"是否改变引用来源。作者希望厘清位置信息通过何种通道进入检索环节，从而判断 GEO 审计是否必须控制位置变量。

Method: 构造 300 个本地与商业类问题，设置 19 个声明位置调用 ChatGPT，并从 5 个网络位置分别调用 ChatGPT 与 Gemini，每个配置重复两次以估计 run-to-run 噪声，共 31,200 条回答；对比引用源集合与推荐商家集合随位置与通道的变化。

Abstract: Generative search engines answer local and commercial questions with a written answer and a few cited sources. It ignores the network. Gemini ignores the declared location and follows the country of the network, changing most of its sources even for questions that do not depend on place. The businesses the answers recommend change with the sources. Thus, visibility in generative search is specific to the user's location, and audits must control both channels.

### [3] [Candidate-dependent residual routing trades top-of-list accuracy for recall in sequential recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24200)
*Han Chen, Yingrui Li*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 排序模型/Ranking | Importance: 低

TL;DR: 在自注意力序列推荐基线上加小型残差路由模块，对比"依赖用户历史表示"与"依赖候选物品embedding"两种路由方式。REES46 数据集五组配对种子实验显示：候选相关路由对 NDCG@10 几乎无影响（+0.003%），但内部存在相互抵消的两种效应——Recall 提升 1.81%，MRR 下降 1.09%，新增命中多落在第 6–10 位。

Ranking Relevance: 直接相关于排序侧序列建模：细化了"target attention / 候选感知注意力"（如 DIN、SIM、ETA 一类)与"user-only attention"的对比，但结论偏负向——在自注意力基线上做残差式候选条件化，收益被 MRR 损失抵消。对工业粗排/精排的启示是：候选条件化注意力本身不保证增益，增益更可能来自长序列检索式选择（如 SIM 的 hard search）、位置偏置与多目标校准；若线上对 top 位精度敏感，应优先关注首位质量而非 top-10 覆盖。样本规模（单数据集、5 seeds、无显著性检验、无线上验证）也限制了外推。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 验证"候选相关路由"(candidate-dependent routing)——即对每个待打分物品用不同权重聚合用户历史行为——是否真能带来排序收益，并将收益来源拆解清楚（是路由机制本身，还是路由权重的输入信号）。

Method: 在自注意力序列推荐基线上加一个小的残差路由模块，形成两版本消融：路由权重分别由（a）用户历史表示、（b）候选物品 embedding 决定。三模型在 REES46 美妆店数据集上做匹配训练，5 个配对随机种子，指标 NDCG@10 为主，辅以 Recall@10、MRR 及按"目标是否落在最近 50 个行为窗口内"的分桶分析。

Result: NDCG@10 平均相对变化 +0.003%（基本持平）；Recall +1.81%，MRR −1.09%；增益集中在用户最近 50 个事件内含目标的 query，目标全部在窗口外的 query 反而变差；新增命中主要分布在 top-10 的第 6–10 位。

Conclusion: 以这种轻量残差形式实现的候选相关路由，本质是在列表顶部用精度换列表下部召回，整体 NDCG 不变。对工业排序系统意味着：若目标指标是 top 位置精度（首位点击、MRR、top-1 转化），该机制大概率无效甚至有害；只有在下游更依赖深度召回/多样性、或截断位置较靠后（如双列瀑布流 6–10 位曝光占比高）的场景才可能获益。此外它天然呈现"长尾/短期兴趣外推能力弱"的退化，需与长序列建模配合。

Abstract: Candidate-dependent routing lets a sequential recommender weight a user's past behaviors differently for each item it scores. We test whether this flexibility helps by adding a small residual routing module to a self-attentive baseline, in two versions that differ only in what sets the routing weights: the user's history representation or the candidate item's embedding. Across five paired seeds on the REES46 cosmetics-shop dataset, with matched training for all three models, candidate-dependent routing leaves the primary metric, normalized discounted cumulative gain at cutoff ten (NDCG@10), essentially unchanged (mean relative change +0.003%). Secondary and exploratory analyses show that this flat average hides two effects that cancel. The candidate-dependent model places more relevant items in its top ten (recall +1.81%) but ranks the first one lower (mean reciprocal rank -1.09%), and most of its new hits land at positions six to ten. Its gains come from queries with at least one target among the user's last 50 events, while queries whose targets all lie outside that window get worse. In this form, candidate-dependent routing trades accuracy at the top of the list for recall further down.
