### [1] [Label-Free Detection of Structural Bias in LLM-Based Recommender Systems via Randomized Paraphrasing: The Case of Verbosity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18878)
*Carlos Peláez-González, David Herrera-Poyatos, Francisco Herrera, Andrés Herrera-Poyatos*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 推荐系统/Recommender System | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 提出一种无需相关性标签的黑盒审计框架：通过对候选商品描述随机分配改写模型并随机排序，构造统计检验，证明若推荐器选择对改写不变，则各改写器被选概率相等；据此在2万条商品查询、5个模型家族9个LLM上量化描述长度（冗长度）偏置，发现控制相关性后长度每增加一个标准差可使被选几率最高提升38%。

Ranking Relevance: 与排序模型间接但重要相关：其一，它刻画了LLM reranker/生成式推荐器对候选side-feature（描述长度）的伪相关敏感性——这正是排序模型“特征泄漏/捷径学习”问题在LLM范式下的再现，提示训练与推理阶段需做长度归一化、改写增强或对抗去偏；其二，其“随机改写+随机排序+等概率零假设”的检验思路可迁移为离线排序评测的去偏工具，替代依赖人工相关性标签的偏置检测；其三，对工业LLM选品/生成式重排，可直接用于上线前的稳定性与公平性回归测试，并指导prompt中候选描述的规范化（长度裁剪、统一模板）以降低secondary signal权重。局限在于其针对单步listwise选品而非完整打分排序链路，且未给出消除偏置的建模方案。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM作为电商/对话式购物推荐器，从自然语言描述的候选中选品，其决策除受查询-商品相关性影响外，可能被描述的表面结构属性（如长度）左右。此类结构性偏置难以检测，因为缺乏相关性标注，且现有评测依赖人工标签，成本高、不可常态化。作者希望提供一种可在部署系统上例行运行的、无需标注的偏置审计手段。

Method: label-free 审计框架：（1）对候选描述随机指派不同paraphrasing模型进行改写，并以随机顺序呈现；（2）理论证明：若推荐器对改写不变（invariance），则每个改写在统计上应被等概率选中，从而给出无需相关性标签的检验统计量；（3）四步统计pipeline：先检验等概率零假设，再在控制查询-商品相关性信号的前提下，量化各描述特征（如长度）对选择概率的边际贡献，输出效应大小与置信区间；（4）仅需黑盒访问、无需任何标注，并给出含成本与样本量建议的审计协议。

Result: 在20,000条商品查询、来自5个模型家族的9个推荐LLM上审计verbosity bias：控制已测量的相关性信号后，描述长度在全部9个模型中均对选择呈正效应，长度每增加一个标准差，被选几率最多提升38%。相关性仍是推荐的首要驱动因素，冗长度作为一致的次级信号，其强度随模型家族而异。

Conclusion: LLM推荐器普遍存在可测的冗长度偏置，且该偏置独立于相关性信号；由于框架仅依赖黑盒访问与无标注数据，可作为已部署购物助手的常规审计工具。对工业界的启示是：需在LLM式选品/生成式召回排序中加入长度归一化、改写不变性正则或位置/长度去偏，并在上线流程中引入此类因果式公平性回归测试。

Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed as recommenders in e-commerce and conversational shopping systems, where they select a product from a set of retrieved candidates described in natural language. Their decisions should depend on relevance to the user query, but may also be influenced by surface-level properties of the descriptions, such as their length. Detecting such structural biases is difficult because relevance labels are rarely available. We propose a label-free framework to audit LLM-based recommender systems for structural bias. Candidate descriptions are rewritten by randomly assigned paraphrasing models and presented in random order. We prove that, if the recommender's choice is invariant to these rewritings, every paraphraser is selected with equal probability, yielding a statistical test that requires no relevance labels. After controlling for the measured relevance signals, description length has a positive effect on selection in all nine models, increasing the odds of selection by up to 38% per standard deviation of length. Relevance remains the primary driver of recommendations, while verbosity acts as a consistent secondary signal whose strength depends on the model family. Since the framework needs only black-box access and no annotations, it can be run as a routine audit of deployed shopping assistants, and we provide an auditing protocol with cost and sample-size guidance.

Furthermore, augmenting existing question-answering pipelines with our retrieved evidence improves answer accuracy by 7.6% on DocScope and 15.8% on FinRAGBench-V, demonstrating the effectiveness of relationship-aware multimodal retrieval.

### [3] [TokenFormer V2: Breaking the Resolution-Complexity Dilemma in Unified Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19074)
*Kaihui Wu, Guoming Li, Liang Guo, Yifeng Zhou, Yuehong Hu, Junwei Pan, Zhixiang Feng, Chengguo Yin, Wenqi Fan, Haijie Gu, Jie Jiang*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 预估模型/CTR-CVR Estimation | Importance: 高

TL;DR: TokenFormer V2 提出统一骨干架构，将"表征分辨率"与"交互密度"解耦：用组级 token 布局编码行为片段与非序列上下文，通过分层组归并扩大交互范围，并以 NS Fusion / Global Sync 协调分布式组，实现局部-全局分解；在 TAAC 2026 榜单以 5.1× 更少 FLOPs 取得最优精度，微信视频号广告离线 +0.07pp AUC，线上 A/B GMV +2.33%。

Ranking Relevance: 直接作用于排序/预估阶段的用户长行为序列建模：行为序列特征是 CTR/CVR 模型最核心的特征，分辨率—复杂度权衡正是精排与粗排(预排)的共性瓶颈。其实验同时覆盖离线精度、FLOPs 效率与线上 GMV，属于"算力受限下的长序列建模"方向，对预排/精排共用骨干、以及长序列在 GPU 推理预算内的部署有直接工程价值；其 token 组化+分层归并的思路也可迁移到多域行为融合与序列特征交互建模。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 工业推荐存在"分辨率—复杂度"困境：稠密长行为序列的精细建模开销大，而压缩后的历史摘要又损失预测证据；希望在同一骨干内、在严格 serving 预算下同时保留细粒度行为证据并控制交互成本。

Method: 将序列切分为行为段并按组(token group)布局，组内编码片段的细粒度信息，组间以分层归并(hierarchical group merging)扩展交互范围；引入 NS Fusion 与 Global Sync 在分布式组之间做信息融合与全局对齐，形成局部—全局分解计算范式；最终用 Full-Resolution Readout 直接读取全部编码事件，保证所有原始 token 可被预测头直接访问，从而在低交互密度下维持高表征分辨率。

Conclusion: 长行为序列建模的关键不是"多堆算力"或"粗暴压缩"，而是把计算投放到结构化交互上、同时保持全分辨率证据可读；该架构可作为工业级可扩展行为建模范式。

Abstract: Industrial recommendation faces a resolution--complexity dilemma: dense interactions make detailed modeling of long behavior histories expensive, while compact history summaries restrict the evidence available to prediction. A unified backbone must preserve rich behavioral details within strict serving budgets. We introduce \textbf{TokenFormer V2}, a unified architecture that explicitly decouples \emph{representation resolution} from \emph{interaction density}. Its group-wise token layout jointly encodes behavior segments with non-sequential context, expands interaction ranges via hierarchical group merging, and coordinates distributed groups through \emph{NS Fusion} and \emph{Global Sync} modules. Through this \emph{local--global decomposition} paradigm, computation is concentrated on structured interactions while preserving fine-grained evidence, ensuring that every encoded event remains directly accessible to the \emph{Full-Resolution Readout} module. On the Tencent Advertising Algorithm Competition 2026 (TAAC 2026) benchmark, TokenFormer V2 achieves top-ranking accuracy among unified recommendation baselines while using \textbf{5.1$\times$ fewer FLOPs} than leading baselines, sustaining its advantage across long histories and imbalanced behavior domains. Furthermore, in billion-parameter evaluations on WeChat Channels advertising, it consistently outperforms state-of-the-art industrial baselines, yielding up to a \textbf{+0.07 pp} overall AUC gain. In live online A/B testing against the production model, TokenFormer V2 delivers a statistically significant \textbf{2.33\% increase in GMV}. These results establish TokenFormer V2 as a battle-tested blueprint for scalable, full-resolution behavior modeling in industrial recommendation.
