### [1] [Extracting Semantics from Cattle Reporting Categories for Data Interoperability and Findability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09381)
*Kassy Raymond, Andrew Hamilton-Wright, Deborah Stacey*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 其他/Other | Importance: 低

TL;DR: 该研究提出一种自底向上的方法，通过提取并分析牲畜数据集中已有的分类术语（年龄、性别、生产用途修饰词）来提升数据的可发现性与互操作性，无需预先建立元数据或标准。

Ranking Relevance: 该论文属于数据工程与语义互操作领域，聚焦牲畜数据的元数据与术语组织，与搜推广系统的排序模型无直接关联。其可提出的间接联系在于：数据规范化与术语语义对齐的思路（如属性归一化、类别映射）在某些特征工程或标签订阅场景中或有借鉴意义，但整体对排序模型研究价值有限。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 牲畜种群数据分散在不同来源，缺乏统一标准，传统方法依赖标准化元数据和本体（如AGROVOC）的crosswalking，成本高且难以覆盖多样化的用户需求。

Method: 以牛类数据为试点，从4个数据源的5个数据集中提取真实报告类别，分析术语的组成与语义（年龄、性别、生产修饰词），与AGROVOC对比。

Result: 发现数据集中已有术语的粒度细于AGROVOC，能捕获后者未涵盖的修饰信息，证明可利用数据内嵌语义实现互操作性。

Conclusion: 相比强制数据集符合既有词汇表，利用数据集中已有术语的语义可提升可发现性与互操作性，同时保留文化和数据集特定术语。

Abstract: Livestock population data disaggregated by age, sex, and production are important inputs to calculations and models that inform our understanding of global health, yet these data are fragmented across disparate sources. Bridging data siloes to improve the findability of data requires interoperability. Conventional approaches to improving the findability and interoperability of data include indexing standardized metadata. However, creating metadata is time and resource-intensive and is often difficult in domains such as livestock, which lack standards that address the needs of broad user groups. When metadata exist, they typically need to be standardized against a pre-existing vocabulary, ontology, or thesaurus, requiring a technique known as `crosswalking'. To overcome issues in the absence of metadata, the lack of standards, and the resource-intensive solutions that currently exist, this study uses a bottom-up approach. By leveraging real-world reporting categories in datasets, the composition and semantics of terms already present in the data were extracted and analyzed. Using cattle data as a pilot, we find the age, sex, and production modifiers present across cattle terms from five datasets from four data sources capture granularity not present in AGROVOC, the largest agricultural vocabulary in the world. We discuss how the composition and semantics of these terms can be used to improve the interoperability and findability of data without first requiring metadata to be generated or standards to be created. Rather than forcing datasets to conform to an existing vocabulary, this approach uses the semantics embedded in terms already present in datasets, allowing systems to make data more discoverable and interoperable while maintaining culturally and dataset-specific terminology.

### [2] [Should I Be Polite to My LLM Relevance Judge? Tone as a Severity Operating-Point Shift](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09703)
*Tian Zhang, Meng Li*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 搜索引擎/Search Engine | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 研究LLM作为相关性评判者时，prompt语气(tone)对标注结果的影响。在3498对TREC DL19/DL20查询-段落上，用8个评判模型、5个礼貌等级、每级3种复述进行实验。发现效果高度依赖模型，语气改变一致性更多是评判者"严重度操作点"偏移而非判断质量提升，且对calibration-based一致性影响大于对排序结果的影响(NDCG@10最大变化仅0.011)。

Ranking Relevance: 与排序模型直接相关度中等，但对搜推广系统有实际意义：(1)越来越多工业系统用LLM-as-judge构建相关性/质量标签用于离线评估与训练数据，本文揭示prompt语气会系统性偏移judge的严格度操作点，意味着标注噪声与评估偏差。(2)区分NDCG@10(排序指标)与calibration一致性，指出语气主要影响绝对标签标定而非Top-K排序，这对用LLM标签做A/B评估或标注训练集尤为重要。(3)提供query-disjoint cross-fit等验证方法，可迁移用于监控judge稳定性。因此对依赖LLM judge的离线评测、标注pipeline和reranking监督信号有警示价值。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM越来越多被用作相关性评判者(relevance judge)以替代或补充人工标注，但其标签会随prompt表层形式(如语气)漂移，威胁标注有效性。作者希望厘清语气这一具体特征如何影响LLM判断，并解释此前研究中互相矛盾的结论。

Method: 控制实验：TREC DL19/DL20共3498对查询-段落，8个judge模型，通过分类器校准出5个礼貌程度(politeness level)，每级3种paraphrase。分析语气变化与human annotator一致性(agreement)的关系，用query-disjoint cross-fit做统计验证(Spearman ρ=-0.683，model-block置换检验p=0.019)，并区分calibration一致性与ranking outcome(NDCG@10、Kendall τ)。

Result: 效果强模型依赖：一个judge呈U型响应，多数仅小变化。语气导致的一致性变化更符合judge"severity operating point"(整体打分宽严)偏移的解释——偏移方向使judge更接近或远离人工标注者的严格度。32组model-tone对比中NDCG@10最大绝对均值变化0.011，但Kendall τ最低到0.743，说明重排序被削弱但未消失。tone对calibration一致性影响大于对排序结果的影响。

Conclusion: prompt语气是LLM相关性判断的一个潜在有效性威胁(validity threat)，尤其在绝对相关性标签重要时。该"severity shift"解释统一了此前矛盾的发现。对依赖LLM judge做绝对标签或离线评估的系统需警惕prompt表层形式的干扰。

Abstract: Large language models are increasingly used as relevance judges, yet their labels can shift with prompt surface form. We study one such feature -- tone -- on 3,498 TREC DL19/DL20 query-passage pairs, across eight judge models, five classifier-calibrated politeness levels, and three paraphrases per level. Effects are strongly model-dependent: one judge shows a structured U-shaped response, whereas most show only small changes. Where tone changes agreement, the results are more consistent with a shift in the judge's severity operating point -- its overall scoring leniency -- than with improved judgment. Agreement rises or falls as this shift moves the judge toward or away from human annotators' strictness. A query-disjoint cross-fit retains the expected association (Spearman $ρ= -0.683$; exact model-block permutation $p = 0.019$). Tone affects calibration-based agreement more than ranking outcomes: across 32 model-tone contrasts, the largest absolute mean change in NDCG@10 is 0.011, although Kendall's $τ$ as low as 0.743 shows that reordering is reduced, not absent. The account reconciles prior contradictory findings and identifies prompt tone as a potential validity threat when absolute relevance labels matter.

### [3] [Purchase Advice and Observable Buyer Responses in Real AI Conversations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09878)
*Benjamin Tannenbaum*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 其他/Other | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 该论文对 AI 助手对话日志中的"购买说服"进行描述性审计，核心结论是测量局限：推荐内容可观测性远高于购买决策结果的可观测性，无法支撑转化率或因果说服效应的估计。

Ranking Relevance: 本文不涉及排序模型、特征交互或召回/重排算法本身，因此与"排序模型"研究的技术关联较弱；但其价值在于为对话式推荐/广告场景的**成效度量与归因**提供警示：日志中的推荐曝光/内容与下游购买决策之间存在严重可观测性缺口，任何基于对话深度或对话内显式意图来近似转化的做法都会产生偏差。这对将生成式助手作为推荐或广告导购入口、并试图用对话信号构建 CTR/CVR 标签或做排序目标优化的工业系统具有直接的方法论参考意义——即需要外部交易/曝光闭环数据来校准，而不能仅依赖会话文本。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 业界与学界普遍关注生成式助手是否能"说服"用户购买，但对话日志虽然包含推荐内容，却未必记录用户后续的购买决策。作者希望厘清：在真实合规的对话数据中，到底能观测到什么、观测不到什么，从而明确"说服/转化"度量本身的边界。

Method: 审计 317 条历史交互（2023年4月—2025年7月），来自 Aiso 专有研究数据库（持证、同意、去标识化的商用 AI 助手对话）。采用单智能体 AI 辅助筛查，标注出指向购买导向的记录；对共享前缀的重复副本做折叠去重；随后统计助手回复形式（候选选项、获取渠道、条件化偏好等）、用户后续轮次出现情况，以及是否出现购后承诺/完成购买/放弃品类等显式陈述。输出为操作化定义、无原文的标注与可复现的描述性结果。

Result: 筛出 68 条购买导向记录，去重后保留 67 个 episode。52 个 episode（77.6%）中助手给出候选选项、渠道或条件化偏好；仅 1 个 episode 出现对具名住宿候选的条件化转向；无任何 episode 被编码为建议放弃或推迟该购买品类。仅 18 个 episode（26.9%）在同一购买任务内出现后续用户轮次，而有任意后续用户轮次的为 23 个（34.3%）——即仅用对话深度会将该后续可用性高估 27.8%。在 47 条保留的用户后续消息中，没有观察到显式的购后承诺、完成购买报告或放弃品类陈述。作者强调这些"零"描述的是记录到的陈述，而非转化率或说服率。

Conclusion: 论文的贡献是测量层面的诊断与可复现的操作化定义：推荐内容的可观测性远高于买家后续决策的可观测性。由于样本为选择性历史样本、AI 标注未经人工验证、且缺失交易结果，该数据无法支撑人群级别或因果层面的说服效应估计。对工业界而言，这提示依赖对话日志做转化归因存在系统性偏差。

Abstract: How often does a generative assistant persuade someone to buy, or persuade them not to buy? Conversation logs contain recommendations, but they do not necessarily record subsequent decisions. We audit 317 historical interactions from Aiso's proprietary research database of licensed, consent-based, de-identified conversations with commercially available AI assistants. Single-agent AI-assisted screening identifies 68 purchase-directed records; collapsing one shared-prefix copy yields 67 retained episodes, dated April 2023 to July 2025. Assistant responses provide candidate options, acquisition channels, or conditional preferences in 52 episodes (77.6%). One episode contains conditional redirection away from a named accommodation candidate. No episode is coded as advice to abandon or defer the purchase category. Only 18 episodes (26.9%) contain a subsequent user turn within the same purchase-related mission, compared with 23 (34.3%) that contain any later user turn. Using conversation depth alone therefore overstates this follow-up availability by 27.8%. Across 47 retained user follow-up messages, no explicit post-advice purchase commitment, completed-purchase report, or purchase-category abandonment statement is observed. These zeros describe recorded statements, not conversion or persuasion rates. The paper supplies operational definitions, text-free annotations, and reproducible descriptive results. Its central finding is a measurement limitation: recommendation content is observable much more often than a buyer's subsequent decision. The selected historical sample, unvalidated AI annotations, and missing transaction outcomes do not support a population-level or causal estimate of persuasion.

### [4] [Guaranteeing Faithful Evidence Extraction in Speculative Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10046)
*Quentin Signé, Mohand Boughanem, Jose Moreno, Thiziri Belkacem*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 其他/Other | Importance: 低

TL;DR: 提出约束式混合解码（CHyD），将推测解码的"快速验证"机制改造为"强制逐字约束解码"，保证RAG输出中引用的span严格来自检索文档原文。

Ranking Relevance: 该工作聚焦RAG生成的忠实性与约束解码，不直接涉及排序模型。与搜推广排序的潜在联系在于：其一，检索结果的证据质量与排序密切相关，"逐字可溯源"可作为对召回/排序文档的新评估维度，进而反哺证据级rerank；其二，约束解码中的草稿—验证机制与预排序中的"低成本草稿+精排验证"思路同源，可启发在生成式推荐/广告文案生成中保证事实一致性；其三，工程上对解码延迟的约束思路可迁移至在线排序的时延预算管理。总体属外围相关，非排序核心贡献。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM作为检索接口易产生幻觉与忠实性错误，生成内容与检索证据不符；现有RAG及半抽取式方法虽缓解问题，但无法保证引用的span逐字来自上下文，在航空维修等安全关键场景中不可接受。

Method: 借用推测解码（speculative decoding）的草稿—验证架构，但目标从加速推理改为忠实性保证：当抽取模式被正确触发时，施加强硬解码约束，将生成限制在检索文档中存在的连续span内，从而保证输出中任何显式引用片段都逐字出现在上下文中。

Result: 在多种SOTA LLM及抽象式、抽取式、半抽取式QA基准（含航空维修等技术数据集）上评估：现有混合方法常出现引用span幻觉，技术领域精确抽取准确率低于40%；CHyD在各类模型上均达到近乎完美的抽取忠实性。硬约束带来流畅度指标上的权衡，但精确答案正确率提升，整体仍具竞争力。

Conclusion: 硬约束解码为安全关键检索场景提供了简单而稳健的忠实性保证；该方法牺牲部分流畅度换取逐字可信度，适合对证据可溯源性要求极高的信息检索应用。

Abstract: Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly used as interfaces for information retrieval, but they remain prone to hallucinations and faithfulness errors, in which the generated answers diverge from the retrieved evidence. While Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and recent hybrid or semi-extractive approaches mitigate this issue, they do not guarantee that quoted or extracted spans are verbatim from the retrieved context. This limitation can have severe consequences in safety-critical domains, where answers must exactly match certified documentation.
  We introduce Constrained Hybrid Decoding (CHyD), a novel faithfulness-first paradigm for speculative RAG. While traditional speculative decoding is optimized for inference speed, CHyD repurposes this architecture to ensure faithful verbatim evidence extraction when the extraction mode is correctly triggered. Our approach enforces hard decoding constraints that restrict generation to continuous spans present in the retrieved documents. This design provides a robust but straightforward guarantee: any explicitly quoted span in the output appears verbatim in the provided context.
  We evaluate our method across state-of-the-art LLMs on diverse abstractive, extractive, and semi-extractive QA benchmarks, including technical datasets motivated by aircraft maintenance. Results show that existing hybrid methods frequently hallucinate quoted spans, with exact extraction accuracy dropping below 40% in technical domains. In contrast, our approach achieves near-perfect extraction faithfulness regardless of the model used. Although enforcing hard constraints introduces a trade-off with fluency-oriented metrics, our method improves exact answer correctness and remains competitive overall, highlighting its suitability for safety-critical information retrieval applications.

### [5] [LiteRAG: Cost-Efficient Graph-Based Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10239)
*Daniel Alejandro Coll Tejeda, Pedro García López, Daniel Barcelona-Pons*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: LiteRAG：用查询条件下的算法化探索与推理链上下文构建替代检索期的LLM控制，实现图检索的高质量、低延迟、低成本多跳问答。

Ranking Relevance: 该工作不涉及排序打分模型，但属于召回/检索阶段优化。其"用查询条件化算法替代LLM控制"的思路对工业搜索与推荐中的图召回（如知识图谱、行为图、兴趣图的多跳扩展）有直接借鉴意义：查询自适应阈值与枢纽节点惩罚可缓解图扩展召回中的"热门节点霸屏、上下文冗余"问题，降低下游排序与生成的输入长度与在线延迟。也可作为RAG类生成式推荐的候选上下文构建模块，对排序前的候选集质量与效率均有正收益。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有基于图的检索（如GraphRAG Global、DRIFT）在多跳问答上有效，但查询时需要大量LLM调用导致延迟与成本极高，且生成的上下文弥散、过大，反而降低生成效率。目标是在保持多跳检索质量的前提下，去除检索期的LLM开销并压缩上下文规模。

Method: LiteRAG将昂贵的检索期LLM控制替换为查询条件化的算法化探索（query-conditioned algorithmic exploration）与推理链上下文构建（reasoning-chain context construction）。关键组件包括查询自适应阈值（query-adaptive thresholding）与社区感知的枢纽节点惩罚（community-aware hub penalization），用于筛选高价值节点、抑制泛化性枢纽带来的上下文膨胀。

Result: 在DistComp多跳检索基准上，LiteRAG综合质量最高（0.798），相比GraphRAG Global与DRIFT单查询延迟降低100倍以上、成本降低99%以上；在UltraDomain上综合质量与LinearRAG持平，但token消耗约减少14倍。消融实验表明查询自适应阈值与社区感知枢纽惩罚是token效率提升的主要来源。

Conclusion: LiteRAG证明：检索期的LLM控制可被查询条件化的图算法替代，在不牺牲多跳检索质量的情况下大幅降低延迟、成本与上下文长度，为图增强检索提供了可工业化的高效范式。

Abstract: Graph-based retrieval can improve multi-hop question answering, but existing approaches often incur high query-time costs and produce diffuse, oversized contexts that reduce generation efficiency. We present LiteRAG, a graph-based retrieval method that replaces expensive retrieval-time LLM control with query-conditioned algorithmic exploration and reasoning-chain context construction. On DistComp, a benchmark for multi-hop retrieval over distributed-systems papers, LiteRAG attains the highest overall quality among the evaluated methods (0.798) while reducing per-query latency by over 100$\times$ and cost by over 99% relative to GraphRAG Global and DRIFT. On UltraDomain, it matches LinearRAG on overall quality while using about 14$\times$ fewer tokens. An ablation study indicates that LiteRAG's query-adaptive thresholding and community-aware hub penalization are the main drivers of its token-efficiency gains.
