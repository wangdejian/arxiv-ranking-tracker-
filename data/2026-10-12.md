### [1] [Beyond the Final Embedding: Retrieving Memories from Token-Level Evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13277)
*Han Luo, Zhan Zhuang, Xu He, Jujia Zhao, Yu Li, Suqin Yuan, Dong Li, Lei Feng*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 向量检索/Vector Retrieval | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 提出免训练的稀疏token匹配检索方法，融合局部token级匹配与整体向量相似度排序记忆，并可选训练低秩query适配器进一步提升效果。

Ranking Relevance: 该方法本质上是一个两阶段式的召回/粗排增强器：先用token级稀疏匹配做候选记忆召回打分，再与稠密整记忆相似度融合进行排序（类似多信号融合的re-rank/pre-rank）。对搜推广系统而言，其"稠密向量+稀疏token匹配混合"思路可直接迁移到召回与预排序阶段——用局部特征交互补充单向量表达的不足，且免训练特性降低落地成本；低秩query适配器相当于轻量query-side个性化/领域适配，与工业界的query tower侧微调、双塔召回优化高度契合。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有embedding检索器主要用单向量表示比较query与记忆，但相关性常仅依赖内容中的一小部分（局部证据），单向量难以捕捉这种细粒度匹配。

Method: 复用embedding模型已计算的上下文token表示，做稀疏token匹配：每个query token检索候选记忆中的少量匹配token，对每个记忆聚合匹配分数，并与整记忆相似度结合排序；可选低秩query适配器在有监督下训练，同时固定encoder与存储的记忆表示。

Result: 在8个基准、多个模型家族上免训练即提升检索；加入query适配后在Qwen3-Embedding-4B上Recall@10和NDCG@10平均提升13.3和14.3个百分点，并转化为长期记忆问答准确率提升。

Conclusion: token级稀疏匹配结合全局相似度是无训练成本的有效检索增强手段，低秩query适配可进一步放大增益，且检索增益能传导到下游QA效果。

Abstract: Memory-augmented assistants retrieve evidence from stored conversations and documents to answer queries. Most embedding-based retrievers compare single-vector representations of queries and memories, even when relevance depends on a small part of the content. Such local evidence can be matched to queries using the contextual token representations already computed by the same embedding models. Our method uses sparse token matching to improve retrieval without additional training. Each query token retrieves a small set of matching tokens across candidate memories. The matching scores are aggregated for each memory and combined with whole-memory similarity to rank memories. An optional low-rank query adapter can be trained with supervision while keeping the encoder and stored memory representations fixed. Our method improves retrieval across eight benchmarks and multiple model families without additional training. Query adaptation further increases the gains to 13.3 and 14.3 percentage points in average Recall@10 and NDCG@10 on Qwen3-Embedding-4B. These retrieval gains translate into higher question-answering accuracy on long-term memory benchmarks.

### [2] [PRIME: Pre-Retrieval Index Modeling Enhancement for Structured Document Retrieval-Real](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13296)
*Cheuk Hei Chung, Li Liu*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 向量检索/Vector Retrieval | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 本文提出索引侧优化框架PRIME，通过LLM驱动的语义重构(SR)与确定性的元数据合成(MS)+层次检索(HR)两条路线优化语料侧表示，在降低索引成本的同时提升检索与回答质量。

Ranking Relevance: 本文面向LLM agent的RAG检索，与搜推广排序模型无直接关系，但其结论对ranking pipeline有重要启示：其一，索引侧/召回过量冗余会影响后续排序收益，向量数与嵌入计算的大幅压缩有助于降低召回层成本；其二，粗到细的层次检索与reranker的关系（索引侧优化在强reranker下中性）印证了"召回质量与精排能力互补、精排足够强时可掩盖召回粗糙"的经典结论，对RAG场景的召回-重排协同设计有参考价值。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有信息检索研究主要优化查询侧检索器(ColBERT、BGE)与生成器(Self-RAG、GraphRAG)，而将语料侧表示简化为分块(chunking)，索引侧质量成为被忽视的优化杠杆。作者认为这是提升LLM agent高精度检索的关键未被充分挖掘的方向。

Method: PRIME包含两条互补路线：(1) LLM驱动的语义重构(SR)，将长文档转化为结构化、信息密集的章节；(2) 确定性的元数据合成(MS)与层次检索(HR)，抽取语义锚点与标题结构，实现粗到细的章节检索。

Result: 确定性元数据达到结构感知分块的检索质量，同时将嵌入计算量降低45.8倍、索引向量数量降低22.4倍；在此基础上SR在全量部署中进一步将Answer-F1提升13.7%，并刻画了质量-保真度-成本权衡。在QASPER、BRIGHT、NFCorpus上总结了决定索引侧优化有益或有害的三种定性机制，并发现这些索引侧效应在强reranker下被抵消。

Conclusion: 索引侧优化是检索系统中被低估的杠杆，元数据合成可显著降低索引成本，语义重构可提升下游答案质量。但索引侧收益依赖具体检索机制，且在强reranker存在时趋于中性，因此需根据系统配置权衡是否投入索引侧优化。

Abstract: High-precision retrieval over external knowledge bases is essential for LLM agents to operate effectively. However, existing Information Retrieval (IR) research primarily optimizes query-side retrievers (e.g., ColBERT, BGE) and generators (e.g., Self-RAG, GraphRAG), while reducing corpus-side representation to chunking. This leaves index-side quality an underexplored lever. We propose Pre-Retrieval Index Modeling & Enhancement (PRIME), an index-side optimization framework built on two complementary tracks: an LLM-driven Semantic Restructuring (SR) track that transforms long documents into structured, information-dense sections, and a deterministic Metadata Synthesis (MS) and Hierarchical Retrieval (HR) track that extracts semantic anchors and heading structure for coarse-to-fine section retrieval. Our evaluations show that deterministic metadata matches structure-aware chunking's retrieval quality while cutting embedding computation by 45.8 $\times$ and index vector count by 22.4 $\times$. On top of these gains, Semantic Restructuring (SR) further improves Answer-F1 by 13.7% in full deployment, with characterized quality-fidelity-cost trade-offs. Across QASPER, BRIGHT, and NFCorpus, we further characterize three qualitative regimes that determine whether index-side optimization helps or degrades retrieval, while showing that these index-side effects are neutralized under a strong reranker.

### [3] [NEAR: Neighborhood-Aware Residuals for Memory Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13314)
*Han Luo, Zhan Zhuang, Xu He, Jujia Zhao, Yu Li, Suqin Yuan, Dong Li, Lei Feng*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 向量检索/Vector Retrieval | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 提出免训练框架NEAR：利用近邻信息构造查询与记忆的残差表示，补充原始嵌入，缓解存储记录间共享内容干扰，提升记忆检索精度。

Ranking Relevance: 直接关联召回/向量检索环节：通过改进嵌入表示缓解相似度失真，属于提升向量召回精度的表示层方法。与排序模型的关系在于，检索阶段的假阳性（错误证据）是后续精排/重排的输入噪声，NEAR可提升候选质量；其"近邻参考+残差"思路对排序特征构造、去偏与候选去冗余亦有借鉴意义。免训练、单索引、单次ANN的特点使其工程落地成本低，适合作为现有双塔召回系统的增强模块。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 记忆检索中，不同存储记录可能共享主题、实体或事件，这些共享内容会让不含所需证据的记录获得较高相似度并排在正确证据之前，导致检索错误。

Method: NEAR为每条记忆从其邻居推导一个参考方向，查询则从预计算表中获取参考；据此为查询与记忆构造投影残差，与原始嵌入组合后在固定记忆索引上做单次近似最近邻搜索；整个过程无需训练。

Result: 在八个长文本检索基准上，跨九种嵌入模型均取得一致提升；下游问答任务性能也得到改善。

Conclusion: 通过近邻感知的残差表示，NEAR在免训练、单次ANN检索的代价下有效抑制共享内容干扰，提升检索与问答表现。

Abstract: Memory retrieval aims to identify evidence from stored records to answer a query. Stored records with different factual details may share topics, entities, or events. Such shared contents would make records without the required evidence more likely to receive high similarity scores and outrank the correct evidence. In this paper, we use the information shared by these records to construct local references for query-memory matching. We propose NEighborhood-Aware Residuals (NEAR), a training-free framework that uses these references to construct residual representations that complement the original embeddings. Specifically, each memory derives a reference direction from its neighbors, and each query obtains its reference from a precomputed table. Using these references, NEAR constructs projection residuals for both queries and memories. These residuals are combined with the original embeddings for a single approximate nearest-neighbor search over a fixed memory index. Experiments on eight long-context retrieval benchmarks show consistent improvements across nine embedding models. Downstream evaluations show that NEAR improves question-answering performance.

</details>


### [4] [SPARQL-LLM in the TEXT2SPARQL 2026 Challenge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13648)
*Panayiotis Smeros, Vincent Emonet, Ruijie Wang, Ana-Claudia Sima, Tarcisio Mendes de Farias*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 搜索引擎/Search Engine | Importance: 低

TL;DR: SPARQL-LLM 是一个开源 KGQA 系统，利用 LLM 将自然语言翻译为 SPARQL，在 TEXT2SPARQL 2026 挑战赛中综合排名第一，比第二名准确率高最多 59%，支持英/德/西多语言，并已部署于生物信息学知识图谱生产环境。

Ranking Relevance: 该工作属于知识图谱问答与语义解析（NL2SPARQL），与搜推广的排序模型关联较弱：它关注查询生成而非候选排序，不涉及 LTR、CTR/CVR 预估、特征交互或多阶段级联架构。潜在连接在于：(1) 结构化知识图谱可作为搜索引擎/推荐系统的知识源，为实体理解、query 改写与语义召回提供支撑；(2) 其 LLM 语义解析思路可迁移到 query 理解与结构化意图识别模块；(3) 在多语言/去中心化场景下的工程实践对构建知识问答类检索产品有参考价值。但整体对工业排序系统的方法论贡献有限。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 分布式知识图谱的 SPARQL 查询门槛高，需要掌握查询语法、端点规范和 schema 细节，普通用户难以直接使用；希望通过自然语言到 SPARQL 的自动翻译降低查询门槛，并借助 LLM 提升翻译的可行性与准确率。

Method: 基于 LLM 构建开放式 KGQA 系统 SPARQL-LLM，将自然语言问题自动翻译为 SPARQL 查询，支持多语言（英、德、西），面向去中心化的生物信息学知识图谱，并以开源形式发布、在实际生产端点（Expasy）上部署。

Result: 在 TEXT2SPARQL 2026 挑战赛中，SPARQL-LLM 在八个参赛方法中综合排名第一，准确率最高比第二名高出 59%，并支持英文、德文、西班牙文的多语言问答。

Conclusion: SPARQL-LLM 证明了 LLM 驱动自然语言到 SPARQL 翻译在真实知识图谱问答场景中的有效性与工程可落地性，已在生产环境稳定运行并开源，为多语言 KGQA 提供了强基线。

Abstract: Querying distributed data represented as Knowledge Graphs (KGs) via SPARQL remains a difficult task, requiring fluency in query syntax, endpoint specifications, and schema details. Knowledge Graph Question Answering (KGQA) systems address this by automatically translating natural language into SPARQL, and recent advances in Large Language Models (LLMs) have made this translation increasingly feasible. In this paper, we describe SPARQL-LLM, our open-source KGQA system, in the context of the TEXT2SPARQL 2026 challenge. In this challenge, SPARQL-LLM ranks first overall among eight competing approaches across all datasets, achieving up to 59% higher accuracy than the second-best approach and supporting multilingual question answering in English, German, and Spanish. SPARQL-LLM is publicly released at https://github.com/sib-swiss/sparql-llm and deployed in production over decentralized bioinformatics KGs at https://www.expasy.org/chat.

### [5] [Tracing and Taming Hallucinated Terms via Subsequence-Association Filtering and Breadth-Aware Selection for LLM Query Expansion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13810)
*Amit Kumar Jaiswal, Sagar Uprety, Prayag Tiwari*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 中

TL;DR: 本文提出SAFE-QE，一种免训练、与检索器无关的查询扩展方法，基于"子序列关联"幻觉理论对候选扩展词打分，并通过约束覆盖最大化选择扩展词集，在保持nDCG@10的同时显著降低注入的无支撑词数量。

Ranking Relevance: 该工作直接影响召回阶段的查询扩展与噪声控制。对工业检索/推荐系统而言，LLM生成扩展词已被广泛用于Query改写与稀疏/稠密召回，但幻觉词会污染倒排与向量召回，尤其损害强检索器。SAFE-QE提供免训练、检索器无关的去噪与选词框架，可在不改动检索器的情况下接入召回pipeline，降低噪声召回对下游排序（精排/粗排）的压力，具有工程落地价值。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM已成为查询扩展的标准来源，但会产生幻觉——注入不存在的实体和离题词，给排序带来噪声，甚至损害强检索器。现有补救方法（语料接地、互相验证、偏好对齐）把幻觉词当作无差别噪声用一致性启发式过滤，缺乏对某词为何出现的机制性解释，也没有"该保留多少词"的原则性准则。

Method: SAFE-QE是免训练、检索器无关的方法。基于"子序列关联"幻觉解释，对每个候选扩展词按其在语料条件下的跨上下文可复现性打分：在随机查询上下文中反复出现但在伪相关集中缺乏支持，则被标记为dominant hallucinatory association的产物并降权。借鉴语言生成中"一致性（无幻觉）vs 广度（覆盖率）"的权衡，将词选择建模为伪相关集上的约束覆盖最大化，保留近似广、但一致且重采样稳定的词集。还提出Hallucinated Expansion Term Rate（HETR）指标，统计无相关文档支持的注入扩展词。

Result: 在TREC和BEIR基准上评估：SAFE-QE在nDCG@10上与生成式和语料引导式扩展无统计显著差异，但每查询仅注入4.3个无支撑词，而Query2doc为27.8、HyDE为57.5；在强学习稀疏检索器上，其他所有扩展方法均退化，SAFE-QE保持不退化。

Conclusion: 忠实性（低幻觉率）可以在不牺牲可测量的排序质量代价下获得；SAFE-QE提供了机制性解释和原则性词数选择准则，优于纯启发式过滤。

Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are now a standard source of query expansion terms but they hallucinate. They inject non existent entities and off-topic terms that add ranking noise and can hurt strong retrievers. Existing remedies such as corpus grounding, mutual verification and preference alignment treat hallucinated terms as an undifferentiated nuisance to be filtered by agreement heuristics, without a mechanistic account of why a given term appears or a principled criterion for how many terms to keep. We propose Subsequence-Association Filtered, breadth-aware query expansion (SAFE-QE), a training-free, retriever agnostic method that draws on the subsequence-association account of hallucination. We score each candidate expansion term by its corpus conditioned cross-context reproducibility, terms that reappear under randomised query contexts yet lack support in the pseudo-relevant set are flagged as products of dominant hallucinatory associations and down-weighted. Drawing on the characterisation of language generation as a trade-off between consistency (no hallucination) and breadth (coverage), we cast term selection as constrained coverage maximisation over the pseudo-relevant set, keeping a set that is approximately broad but consistent and stable across resampling. We also introduce the Hallucinated Expansion Term Rate, a query side faithfulness metric that counts injected expansion terms no relevant document supports. We evaluate our approach on benchmarked TREC and BEIR collections. Our SAFE-QE is statistically indistinguishable from generative and corpus-steered expansion on nDCG@10 while injecting fewer unsupported terms 4.3 per query against 27.8 for Query2doc and 57.5 for HyDE and on a strong learned sparse retriever, where every expansion method regresses. Faithfulness is therefore available at no measurable cost in ranking quality.

</details>
