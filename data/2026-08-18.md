### [1] [Do AI chatbots find what experts would? Effects of model, user role, and sample size on study retrieval for medical questions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13786)
*Qingfang Liu, Qiao Jin, Joe D. Menke, Thorsten Kahnt, Zhiyong Lu*
