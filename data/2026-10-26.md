Motivation: 标准自回归生成式推荐以 trie 约束 beam search 逐码生成 SID，存在两处 K 倍开销：历史 T 个物品以 TK 个码 token 进入主干；每个 beam step 都要过主干，返回 B 个候选的代价为 O(BK) 主干解码 token/用户。其中第二项通常占主导，且随主干规模、SID 深度与候选预算增长，成为线上大模型大候选池部署的瓶颈。

Method: 1) 历史侧：每个历史物品表示为单个 fused token（而非 K 个码 token），主干一次前向将历史压缩为紧凑的 next-item concept 向量；2) 生成侧：以 concept 为条件、配合已生成前缀的 KV-cache，由轻量解码器独立执行完整 trie-constrained beam search；3) 训练：仅用交叉熵即可逼近全主干精度，再以全主干教师做蒸馏进一步提升；4) SID 词表、前缀树结构与自回归因子分解均保持不变，属即插即用改造。

Result: 在电商、电影、音乐流媒体共 7 个数据集上，精度与标准生成式推荐器及融合输入的 full-backbone teacher 相当或更优；在生产风格服务管线、50ms p95 延迟约束下吞吐提升 3.2–8.6×。Deco 生成 1000 个候选比教师生成 100 个更快；以 1.5B 参数 LLM 为主干时，吞吐与 69M 教师持平而精度更高。

Conclusion: 生成式召回的计算瓶颈可从“主干承载解码”转移为“主干只负责读历史”，用概念瓶颈 + 轻量解耦解码器换取候选预算与模型规模的双重扩展性，为大候选池、深 SID、大主干的工业化生成式检索提供了可行路径。

Abstract: Generative recommenders retrieve items by writing their semantic IDs (SIDs) code by code under trie-constrained beam search, an approach now deployed on large web platforms. Standard autoregressive interfaces pay for the SID length K twice: a history of T items enters the backbone as TK code tokens, and every beam-search step runs through the backbone, so returning B candidates costs O(BK) backbone decode tokens per user. The second cost often dominates, and it grows with the backbone, the SID depth and the candidate budget.
  We introduce Deco (decoding from concepts), which separates reading the history from writing the identifier. The backbone reads each history item as one fused token and condenses the history once into a compact next-item concept; a light KV-cached decoder conditioned on the concept and the generated prefix runs the entire beam search, while the identifiers, the trie and the autoregressive factorization stay unchanged. The decoder approaches full-backbone accuracy with cross-entropy alone, and distillation improves it further. On seven datasets from e-commerce, movies and music streaming, Deco achieves comparable or better accuracy than both a standard generative recommender and the full-backbone teacher with fused inputs, while delivering 3.2--8.6$\times$ their throughput under a 50 ms p95 latency budget in a production-style serving pipeline. Delegating beam search makes larger candidate pools and models practical: Deco generates 1000 candidates faster than its teacher generates 100, and with a 1.5B-parameter LLM backbone, it matches the 69M-parameter teacher's throughput while achieving higher accuracy.
