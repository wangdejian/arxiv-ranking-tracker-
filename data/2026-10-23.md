### [1] [IRAH: Hierarchical Agentic Tree Search for Retrieval Pipeline Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30167)
*Maria Ana Cardei, Aniket Singh, Amir Moghadam, Srividhya Baikadi, Rashmi Shetty*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 搜索引擎/Search Engine | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 提出 IRAH(Information Retrieval Agentic Harness),将 LATS-IR(基于 UCT 的语言智能体树搜索)与离线元学习初始化结合,对 RAG 检索管线的宏观结构与数值超参进行分层联合优化,在有限评测预算下优于 TPE 与冷启动基线。

Ranking Relevance: 论文优化对象是检索侧管线(召回/重排组件的选择与超参),其目标函数为排序质量 nDCG@10,属于“排序度量驱动的系统级 AutoML”,与排序模型的关系是间接的:不提出新的打分/特征交互/多任务结构,而是把排序指标作为黑盒反馈来搜索管线配置。对工业搜推广的潜在价值在于:(1) 多路召回 + 预排 + 重排的分层结构与超参调优本身是标准工程痛点,UCT 分层搜索天然匹配“先结构后参数”的决策顺序;(2) 自然语言 reflection 形成可审查轨迹,便于人工审核与知识沉淀;(3) 元学习 warm-start 可在多业务/多场景间迁移,降低新场景冷启动的离线评测与线上实验成本。局限在于:预算受限下的离线 nDCG 优化与线上 A/B 收益之间仍存在偏差,且 OOD 增益不明显,难以直接替代现有的参数调优与实验体系。

Motivation: RAG 的检索管线优化需同时决定结构组件(如是否加多路召回、重排、查询改写)与数值超参,搜索空间异构、评测预算(标注/调用成本)极度受限;贝叶斯优化(TPE)难以利用结构先验与语言语义信息。

Abstract: Optimizing Information Retrieval (IR) pipelines for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) requires jointly selecting structural components and numerical hyperparameters under limited evaluation budgets. Under the same meta-learned initialization, LATS-IR outperforms TPE on two of three in-distribution benchmarks, with gains of up to 6.4%, and remains competitive on the out-of-distribution benchmark. Across all four benchmarks, warm-started IRAH achieves the highest average held-out nDCG@10 (0.4816) and improves over its cold-start counterpart. These results establish IRAH as a competitive approach to retrieval pipeline optimization under constrained evaluation budgets.

### [3] [FlexVDR: Learning to Allocate Matryoshka Representation Budgets for Visual Document Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30177)

Experiments across five backbones on ViDoRe V1, V2, and V3 public show that FlexVDR retains much of the native retrieval quality while selecting compact routes for individual queries. A configurable resource preference allows the same retriever to adjust its computation without retraining or switching models.
