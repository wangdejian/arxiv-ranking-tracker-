### [1] [ERA: Embed, Route, Adapt for Cold-Start Cross-Model Latent Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26919)
*Venkata Chivukula, Zifeng Wang, Jinsung Yoon*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: ERA 提出"嵌入-路由-适配"的冷启动跨模型隐空间检索系统：用余弦最远点采样（CFPS）挑选少量锚点文档，用流形对齐损失训练轻量适配器，并配确定性路由器将困难查询升级到目标编码器，在 BEIR 上最高提升 35% nDCG@10，并将端到端查询延迟从约 160ms 降至 38-86ms。

Ranking Relevance: 直接面向召回侧（双塔/向量检索）的索引-查询空间错配问题：它规避了向量召回中最昂贵的全量重嵌入，对"换模型/多模型共存/冷启动无日志"的工业场景（如新物料库、跨模态升级、A/B 换 encoder）有实际价值。与排序模型的关系是间接但重要的：路由器的"难查询升级"本质是查询级级联/预路由（类似 pre-ranking 的轻量-精算分层），其确定性升级策略可直接借鉴到排序链路的模型级联与查询难度感知；同时跨空间查询映射思路也可用于多版本排序模型的特征/表示迁移。锚点采样对应工业上"用少量样本对齐新旧表示"的蒸馏式冷启动，可与向量召回、粗排共用一套"廉价近似 + 少量精算"的体系。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 生产检索系统与建库时的嵌入模型深度耦合，换编码器需重嵌入全量语料；冷启动阶段无查询日志，且新编码器对每篇文档嵌入都有 API/算力成本。核心诉求是：在不重嵌入语料、无查询日志的前提下，把查询侧迁移到新编码器并保持检索质量。

Method: 1) 锚点选择：在已有目标索引上以 Cosine Farthest Point Sampling 选取信息量最大的少量文档作为锚点，控制冷启动嵌入预算；2) 适配器训练：以 Manifold Alignment Loss（方向对齐 + 正交正则防表示坍缩）训练轻量适配器，把新编码器查询空间映射到旧索引空间；3) 确定性路由器：将少量"难查询"升级到目标编码器精算，宣称升级集中在投影失效的查询上。

Result: 五个 BEIR 数据集上匹配或超过 point-wise L2 与 embedding-conversion 基线，Arguana 上 nDCG@10 相对提升最高 35%；聚类语料上锚点选择在固定预算下也能改进既有方法。适配器+路由器额外开销 1.2ms（本地编码器约 20ms），在 20% 名义升级预算下，端到端查询延迟由约 160ms 降至 38-86ms。

Conclusion: 论文给出一条低成本、可渐进演进的索引侧迁移路径：以锚点对齐 + 查询级路由避免全量重嵌入，兼顾质量与延迟。但其价值高度依赖路由器的判定质量与锚点对语料的覆盖性，在超大规模、多峰分布语料上的可扩展性与离线/在线一致性仍需工业验证。

Abstract: Production retrieval systems are tied to the embedding model that indexed their corpus. Moving queries to a different encoder normally requires re-embedding the whole corpus, and at cold start there are no query logs while every document embedded by the new encoder incurs an API or compute cost. We present ERA (Embed, Route, Adapt), a system for cold-start cross-model latent retrieval. ERA spends a small anchor budget on maximally informative documents, selected from the existing target index via Cosine Farthest Point Sampling, trains a lightweight adapter with a Manifold Alignment Loss that couples directional alignment with orthogonal regularization to prevent representation collapse, and pairs the adapter with a deterministic router that escalates the rare, hard queries that define perceived retrieval quality. Across five BEIR datasets, ERA matches or outperforms point-wise L2 and embedding-conversion baselines, with relative gains of up to 35% nDCG@10 on Arguana, and on clustered corpora its anchor selection also improves prior methods under a fixed anchor budget. With 1.2 ms of adapter and router overhead on top of a ~20 ms local encoder, ERA at a 20% nominal escalation budget reduces end-to-end query latency from roughly 160 ms to 38-86 ms while concentrating escalations on the queries where the projection fails most.

### [2] [Leader Finding: A New Variation of Expert Finding in Community Question Answering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26921)
*Sepideh Entezari Maleki, Mahmood Neshati*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 搜索引擎/Search Engine | Importance: 低

TL;DR: 论文研究"领队发现"(leader finding)问题：以项目+团队为查询,输出候选领队的排序列表,用概率框架融合技术匹配度、管理知识、与团队沟通三项质量,并为每项提出评估指标。在两个Stack Overflow构建的大规模集合上,最优配置的NDCG@10相比最强基线提升一倍以上。

Ranking Relevance: 属于专家/人员检索这一垂直搜索场景,其"多质量因子概率融合+分维度指标"思路对通用排序有方法论借鉴:即相关性不应是单一标量,而应由多个可解释子目标加权(类似多任务/多目标排序中的label fusion与metric设计)。但技术方案较为传统(概率生成模型,无深度特征交互/序列建模),与主流搜推广主排序链路(CTR/CVR多任务、特征交叉、粗排精排)差异较大,离工业级大规模排序系统较远,故对排序模型研究的直接影响力有限。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统专家检索(expert finding)只按话题专精度排序人,但真实岗位(如团队领队)需要多重素质:懂项目技术但不能过度专精(overqualified)、具备管理知识、能与团队有效沟通。单一话题相关性无法刻画这种多维权衡,因此需要重新定义排序目标。

Method: 把领队发现建模为概率排序框架,将三项质量(技术匹配、管理知识、团队沟通)作为潜在因子组合成相关性概率;对每项质量设计独立评估指标;在Stack Overflow数据上构建两个大规模评测集合,对比多种配置与基线。

Result: 最优配置模型的NDCG@10达到最强基线的两倍以上,说明多质量融合排序显著优于仅依赖话题专精的专家检索方法。

Conclusion: 有效的领队排序必须平衡多种互补质量;话题专精只是其中一维,甚至存在"过度专精"的负向效应。多因子概率建模+分维度评测是可行路径。

Abstract: Expert finding ranks people by their knowledge of a given topic. Many job positions need more than topical expertise, and a team leader is a clear case. A suitable leader knows the technical areas of the project without being overqualified, has strong managerial knowledge, and communicates well with the team. We study the leader finding problem, where the query is a project and a team and the output is a ranked list of candidate leaders. We model the problem in a probabilistic framework that combines technical fit, managerial knowledge, and communication with the team, and we propose one evaluation metric for each of these qualities. On two large collections built from Stack Overflow, the best configuration of our model more than doubles the NDCG@10 of the strongest baseline. These results show that a ranking of leaders must balance several qualities that topical expertise alone does not capture.

### [3] [Deep Cross-Division Network for Product-and-Ratio Feature Interactions in Industrial Ranking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27514)
*Mikhail Trapeznikov, Sofia Gulevskaia, Maksim Utushkin, Aleksandr Poslavsky, Alexander D'yakonov*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 排序模型/Ranking | Importance: 高/High

TL;DR: DCDN 在 DCNv2 风格交叉层中加入除法分支，使网络能直接学习乘积、比值及混合交互，可表达为多变量 Laurent 多项式；线上替换 DCNv2 后消除全部人工比值特征（输入特征减少 40.1%），A/B 实验点赞 +3.25%、分享 +16.26%。

Ranking Relevance: 直接相关：属于排序模型中的特征交互结构创新，是 DCN/DCNv2 家族的直接扩展，可即插即用地替换精排中的 cross network。工业意义明确——用结构化除法分支替代人工 ratio 特征，既降低特征管道复杂度（特征数 -40.1%）又提升线上一致性；对依赖占比类特征（点击率、完播率、转化率、人均计数、曝光归一化等）的 CTR/CVR 预估与多任务排序场景有普适借鉴价值。理论上的 Laurent 多项式刻画给了"层数→可表达阶数/次数"的清晰设计准则，便于在表达能力与延迟之间做取舍。值得注意的是绝对值增益（TVT +0.33%）虽显著但幅度有限，属于典型"工程收益型"论文，落地收益更多来自特征工程简化与非线性比率关系的自动发现。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: DCN/DCNv2 的交叉层只用输入特征的非负整数次幂构造交互，最高阶受层数限制，因此比率、占比、归一化计数这类倒数项无法直接表达，工业界只能人工构造 ratio 特征，带来特征工程量、维护成本与泛化风险。作者希望让模型结构本身具备除法/比值建模能力。

Method: 将 DCNv2 交叉层扩展为含除法分支的结构：在对偶/并行的分支上引入带负指数的特征变换，使 K 层 DCDN 生成 ℓ1-阶不超过 K+1、带符号次数在 [-K, K+1] 之间的 Laurent 单项式，从而联合学习乘积、比值与乘积-比值混合交互。理论部分给出可表示交互的 Laurent 多项式刻画与阶数/次数界。实验上与以 DCNv2 为核心、并手工提供 ratio 特征的生产级神经排序基线对比，用 DCDN 模块整体替换 DCNv2 block。

Result: 相比量产优化的 DCNv2+人工比值特征基线，替换为 DCDN 后去掉了整类人工比值特征，输入特征数下降 40.1%，模型效果不降反升。已在头部短视频服务生产排序链路部署，线上 A/B：点赞 +3.25%、分享 +16.26%、总观看时长(TVT) +0.33%、点击进入全屏 +0.38%（均统计显著），展示视频数小幅下降 -0.24%。代码已开源。

Conclusion: 显式建模除法/比值交互是对乘性特征交叉范式的自然补全：DCDN 用结构先验替代人工 ratio 特征工程，在等量或少量的特征输入下带来显著的用户参与度提升。其价值在于"降维+提效"同时成立，适合作为 DCNv2 的直接替代模块；但整体增益量级（TVT +0.33%）说明主要收益集中在少数强分享/强互动场景，且需注意短视视频数与时长之间的权衡关系。后续可关注归一化计数在训练稳定性上的影响、以及在精排之外（预排/粗排）的算力开销。

Abstract: Learning effective feature interactions is essential for industrial recommender systems. DCN and DCNv2 efficiently automate polynomial feature crossing. However, their cross layers construct interactions using only non-negative integer powers of the input features, with the maximum degree controlled by network depth. Consequently, they cannot directly represent the reciprocal terms required for ratios, rates, and normalized counts unless such features are explicitly provided as inputs. We introduce DCDN, a Deep Cross-Division Network that augments a DCNv2-style cross layer with a division branch. DCDN jointly learns product, ratio, and mixed product-ratio interactions. We characterize the representable interactions as multivariate Laurent polynomials and show that a $K$-layer DCDN generates Laurent monomials of $\ell_1$-order up to $K+1$, with signed degree between $-K$ and $K+1$. We evaluate DCDN against a production-optimized neural-ranking baseline built around DCNv2 and supplied with manually engineered ratio features. Replacing the DCNv2 block with DCDN allowed us to eliminate the entire class of manually engineered ratio features from the model input, reducing the number of input features by $40.1\%$. DCDN is deployed in the production ranking stack of a major short-video service. In a live A/B test on this service, DCDN increased likes by $3.25\%$, shares by $16.26\%$, total view time (TVT) by $0.33\%$, and clicks to open fullscreen by $0.38\%$; all changes were statistically significant. The number of shown videos decreased slightly ($-0.24\%$). These results demonstrate that DCDN can improve user engagement while eliminating reliance on manually engineered ratio features. Our code is publicly released at https://github.com/rw404/DCDN.

### [4] [Decision Columns as an Index: Interpretable Embeddings for Explainable Entity Matching and Search](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27910)
*Aadharsh Kannan, Robert Bruckner, Philip A. Bernstein*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 向量检索/Vector Retrieval | Importance: 中

TL;DR: 提出「可解释嵌入」：用有序问题集合对每条记录编码为 yes/no/not stated 三值决策列，相似度可精确分解到每个问题，新增属性只需追加坐标而无需重编码；在商品实体匹配与检索任务上与稠密向量相当，并可通过小网络从已有稠密向量低成本回填。

Ranking Relevance: 对应排序/检索系统的召回与候选生成阶段：以符号化、可加的属性谓词向量替代/补充稠密向量，天然支持属性过滤（filtered retrieval）与硬约束特征，利于多通道召回融合与可审计重排解释；短问题序列的语义容量有限，适合作为低延迟、可过滤的旁路召回通道或特征源，主流主干排序仍应由稠密双塔+精排承担。工程价值集中在索引可增量扩展（无重编码）、失败驱动的问题生成（类似badcase闭环）、以及用现有嵌入蒸馏回填降低上线成本，对工业可解释性与迭代效率提升有实际意义。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 业务数据系统中，记录判同（entity matching）与隐式属性检索都需要可审计的理由；稠密嵌入效果好但坐标不透明、无法按语义过滤、新增属性需对全量语料重新编码。

Method: 1）决策列嵌入：每列存一个决策模型对自然语言问题的校准答案（yes/no/not stated），记录嵌入=按序回答向量；2）相似度按问题维度精确可加分解，形成可解释贡献；3）新问题直接追加坐标，不改动旧维度；4）用小型神经网络从现有稠密嵌入预测完整可解释向量，实现约1/200成本的语料回填；5）因子式流程：嵌入式候选生成→验证器；检索侧引入「推理合适结果应具备的属性」。

Result: 公开商品基准上，问题空间检索的候选生成效果与稠密检索在统计上持平，且对接受的匹配给出精确解释；由检索失败反推的新问题使MRR提升3倍且无需重编码；检索中推断结果属性优于仅编码字面请求；问题空间与稠密分数融合有提升但未达预设置信阈值。

Conclusion: 可解释嵌入可作为互补、可读的召回通道，用于解释匹配、按属性过滤并随失败发现的缺失事实增量扩展索引；定位为与稠密检索并行的通道而非替代方案。

Abstract: Business data systems must determine whether records refer to the same entity and retrieve records satisfying requests whose decisive properties are implicit. As agents act on these records, both tasks require auditable reasons. Dense embeddings perform well but offer opaque coordinates, cannot be filtered by meaning, and require corpus re-encoding to add new properties. We propose interpretable embeddings built from decision columns: each column stores a decision model's calibrated answer to a natural-language question, with outcomes yes, no, or not stated. A record's embedding is its answers across an ordered set of questions. Similarity decomposes exactly into per-question contributions, and new questions add coordinates without altering existing ones.
  On public product benchmarks, question-space retrieval matches dense candidate generation statistically in a retrieve-then-verify entity matcher, while providing exact explanations for accepted matches. Questions derived from retrieval failures triple mean reciprocal rank without re-encoding prior answers. For search, inferring the attributes of a suitable result outperforms encoding only the literal request, and combining question-space and dense scores improves retrieval, though not at our prespecified confidence threshold. A single small neural network predicts the full interpretable vector from an existing dense embedding, enabling corpus backfilling from a few thousand labeled rows at roughly 1/200 the cost. We position interpretable embeddings as a complementary, readable retrieval channel for explaining matches, filtering on attributes, and extending indexes as failures expose missing facts.
