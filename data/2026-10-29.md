### [1] [RELAY: One Query, Two Roles for Sequential Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36000)
*Zesheng Li, Chengchang Pan, Honggang Qi*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 推荐系统/Recommender System | Importance: 中

TL;DR: RELAY 用一个学习到的 query 同时承担"语义偏好读出"和"行为记忆路由"两种角色：attention 既形成序列偏好表示，又直接路由直连历史与转移证据到候选项打分；候选条件门控校准两路记忆通道；单次前向完成全目录排序，在六个数据集上 Recall@5/NDCG@5 全面最优，预测器参数量减少 65%、推理加速 1.75~1.79 倍。

Ranking Relevance: 属于粗排/召回-精排之间轻量序列建模层：把用户序列编码器与候选项打分器合并，共享注意力证据，直接服务全目录打分（full-catalog softmax），对应工业上"轻量序列建模 + 全库打分"的 pre-ranking/粗排角色。对排序模型的启示有三点：(1) 表示与打分共享证据可消除 target attention 的二次打分开销，天然适合低延迟链路；(2) 参数与算力受限场景下（端上、粗排、实时链路）2M 参数级模型有落地价值；(3) 局限在于仍是单目标全目录似然，未覆盖多任务（CTR/CVR）、特征交叉、粗排-精排级联校准等工业核心问题，且未报告线上时延与分布漂移表现。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 序列推荐需同时建模语义偏好与行为关联，并满足低推理成本。已有方法中偏好表示与候选项打分所依赖的行为证据是割裂的（打分阶段另起模块做 target attention / 手工特征），既增加参数与延迟，也让"用于表示的历史"和"用于打分的历史"不一致。

Method: 核心为"one query, two roles"：共享历史选择（shared history selection）把偏好表示与打分证据统一到同一组注意力权重上。(1) 单一可学习 query 对历史做注意力，得到语义偏好向量；(2) 复用同一注意力权重，将 direct-history 与 transition 两路记忆证据路由到候选项分数；(3) 候选条件门控（candidate-conditioned gate）按候选项动态校准两通道权重；(4) 以单一全目录似然（catalog likelihood，全 softmax / sampled softmax）端到端训练两个角色，单次前向输出完整排名。

Result: 在六个数据集的全目录评测下，Recall@5 与 NDCG@5 均为最优；相对此前最佳模型平均提升 14.22% 与 17.88%。预测器仅 2.044M 参数，较基线减少 65.12%，推理提速 1.75~1.79×。

Conclusion: RELAY 表明"注意力复用 + 表示与打分共享证据"可以在不牺牲精度的情况下大幅压缩模型并降低延迟，为序列推荐提供了一个高性价比的统一范式。

Abstract: Sequential recommendation must combine semantic preferences with behavioral associations to deliver accurate rankings at low inference cost. Sharing history selection connects preference representation directly to the behavioral evidence used in candidate scoring. We present RELAY, a compact sequential recommender built around one query, two roles: semantic readout and behavioral-memory routing. A single learned query forms a semantic preference representation and reuses its attention to route direct-history and transition evidence into candidate scores. Candidate-conditioned gates calibrate the two memory channels for each candidate. RELAY trains both roles with a single catalog likelihood and produces the final ranking in one forward pass. Full-catalog experiments on six datasets show that RELAY achieves the highest Recall@5 and NDCG@5 among the compared methods on every dataset, improving over the previous best model by 14.22% and 17.88% on average. With 2.044 million predictor parameters, RELAY reduces predictor size by 65.12% and achieves a 1.75~1.79$\times$ inference speedup over the previous best model.

### [2] [When Connectivity Misleads: Guiding Agentic Search with Contrastive Evidence Paths](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36134)
*Junchen Li, Liang Xu, Rongzheng Wang, Qizhi Chen, Junhao Wen, Shuang Liang*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 搜索引擎/Search Engine | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 提出 Contrastive Evidence Paths (CEP)：在查询条件化的文档图上，用“最强单条证据路径”为候选打分，并以同图的背景访问分布做校准以抑制结构偏置；进而构建 Structured Corpus Interaction (SCI)，将候选及连接路径组织为结构化 Map 供智能体核验与扩展探索，在 BrowseComp-Plus 与 TREC-COVID 上取得最佳答案准确率与 nDCG@10，且工具调用与生成成本显著下降。

Ranking Relevance: 与排序模型的相关性主要体现在“图结构证据下的候选打分与去偏”：CEP 可视为一种轻量的重排/预打分算子（路径级特征 + 背景分布校准），与工业界常用的 PPR/图扩散式相关度先验形成对照，提示在基于知识图谱或文档引用图的召回-重排链路中，“路径级最强证据 + 对比校准”比度数聚合更能抵抗热门/高连通节点带来的排序虚高；其路径保留与结构化 Map 机制亦可迁移到多跳 RAG 与智能体检索的动作选择/候选窗口构造，用于降低工具调用与 LLM 生成成本。但该方法依赖 LLM 智能体闭环，不直接等价于传统 CTR/CVR 精排，工业落地价值更多在检索侧（图召回、证据链重排、reranker 的上下文构造）而非深度精排建模。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 搜索智能体依赖跨文档图进行多跳发现，但图中高连接度的干扰节点因更易被触达而排名虚高（结构偏置），PPR 等图扩散方法会把多源传播贡献聚合，使这类节点挤占有限观测窗口；同时扁平的节点分数丢失了跨文档证据链，削弱后续探索的连贯性。

Method: 1) 构建查询条件化文档图；2) 对每个候选计算最强单条路径分数（而非聚合多路径贡献），避免“连接多即高分”；3) 用同一图的背景访问分布对路径分数做校准（对比式），抑制度/可达性带来的结构偏置；4) 按校准分排序并保留对应源路径；5) 在此基础上构建 SCI：将候选与路径组织成结构化 Map，智能体可在锚点文档处核验证据并向缺失信息方向扩展。

Result: 在 BrowseComp-Plus 与 TREC-COVID 上，SCI 的答案准确率与 nDCG@10 均达 SOTA，同时生成成本最低；相较最强基线，工具调用次数与总生成成本的最大降幅分别超过 50% 与 40%；分析显示 CEP 有效抑制了结构连接导致的排名通胀，并提升金标召回与探索效率。

Conclusion: 在带图结构的检索/智能体搜索场景中，“单路径对比校准 + 路径保留 + 结构化交互”优于传统图扩散聚合排序，能在更少工具调用与生成开销下提升排序质量与最终答案正确性，对图增强检索与多步搜索智能体的候选打分与探索调度具有借鉴意义。

Abstract: Search agents can use cross-document graphs to discover sources through multi-hop connections and choose subsequent actions based on candidate rankings and relationships within a limited observation window. However, documents may rank highly because their numerous connections make them easier to reach, without necessarily providing more valuable evidence. Graph diffusion methods such as Personalized PageRank (PPR) aggregate propagation contributions from multiple sources, allowing highly connected distractor nodes to crowd the observation window. Flat node scores also obscure cross-document evidence trails, limiting continued exploration. We propose Contrastive Evidence Paths (CEP), which scores candidates by their strongest individual paths on a query-conditioned document graph and calibrates these scores against the same graph's background visitation distribution to mitigate structural bias. CEP ranks candidates by the calibrated scores and retains the corresponding source paths. Building on CEP, we develop Structured Corpus Interaction (SCI), which organizes candidates and their connecting paths into a structured Map, enabling agents to verify source documents and pursue missing information through expansion from anchor documents. Experiments on BrowseComp-Plus and TREC-COVID show that SCI achieves state-of-the-art answer accuracy and nDCG@10 with the lowest generation cost. Compared with the strongest baselines by task performance, maximum reductions in tool calls and total generation costs exceed 50% and 40%, respectively. Further analysis confirms that SCI with CEP suppresses ranking inflation induced by structural connections and improves gold recall and exploration efficiency.
