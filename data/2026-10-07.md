### [1] [Beyond Successor Accuracy: State Retention for Recursive Self-Improvement in Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07105)
*Jinfeng Xu, Zheyu Chen, Ziyue Peng, Zheng Lin, Wenhao Yuan, Jian Chen, Shujie Li, Edith Ngai*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 推荐系统/Recommender System | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 论文提出"分布式进展"(distributed progress)概念并以跨代优势(CGA)量化，指出推荐递归自我改进(Rec-RSI)中以最新模型为唯一产出的评估方式会忽略代际间互补的排序决策，并提出秩分离统计量(rank separation)在无标签下预测应保留哪一代模型。

Ranking Relevance: 与排序模型高度相关：1) CGA是配对式(pairwise)偏好/序关系度量，本质是对排序决策的对比评估，思路可迁移到排序模型的离线评测与A/B之外的代际对比。2) "保留哪一代模型"属于模型选择/状态管理问题，对工业界持续训练(continual training)与多版本模型治理有直接启发：工业迭代中常假设新版本单调变优，本文提示应保留跨代互补的排序能力，而非只上线最新版本。3) rank separation为无标签在线选型提供了轻量信号，可用于流式训练中的模型切换决策。局限：实验限于序列推荐编码器与公开数据集，未涉及大规模CTR/CVR预估与在线指标，对推荐精排/广告系统的落地影响偏方法论层面。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Rec-RSI将推荐输出回喂到后续训练，形成多轮迭代。现有做法默认"后继模型"整合了前代的全部收益，因此只评估/部署最新一轮模型。但前后代模型可能保有互补的排序决策，进步分散在多代之间，仅看最新模型会低估甚至损失性能。作者将此称为distributed progress，并提出状态保留(state retention)作为Rec-RSI中的独立问题。

Method: 1) 定义 distributed progress，并用跨代优势CGA度量：在边缘匹配(marginally matched)条件下对比跨代模型对与同代模型对的差异，以剔除配对样本分布差异的干扰。2) 提出rank separation统计量，用于在无标签的选型时刻预测哪一"家族"(同代配对 vs 跨代配对)更优，从而决定保留策略。3) 在4个数据集、3种序列推荐编码器(GRU4Rec、SASRec、FMLP)上做多轮(第一、第二次更新)实验，并与5种迁移机制(transfer mechanisms，如参数迁移、蒸馏类)作对比。

Result: 保留机制偏好随架构而异：跨代配对对GRU4Rec与SASRec有利；FMLP初次更新时偏好同代配对，第二次更新后转向跨代配对。rank separation在首次更新的12/12、第二次更新的5/6个"数据集-编码器"设置中选出更强家族；在留出测试上，所选家族在34/36条轨迹中优于直接后继模型。而5种迁移机制都无法在单一模型中稳定复现这些收益。

Conclusion: 进步可能存在于代际"关系"中而非仅存在于最新模型里，故Rec-RSI的状态保留应作为独立问题研究；简单地用迁移机制把多代知识压进单一模型并不能稳定等价于跨代配对带来的收益。

Abstract: Recommendation recursive self-improvement (Rec-RSI) feeds recommender outputs into subsequent training. Evaluating each round solely through its latest model assumes that the successor consolidates the update, although pre- and post-update models may retain complementary ranking decisions. We term this \emph{distributed progress} and quantify it using cross-generation advantage (CGA), a marginally matched contrast between cross- and within-generation model pairs. A rank-separation statistic, label-free at selection time, predicts which family to retain. Across four datasets and three sequential recommendation encoders, the preferred retention regime varies by architecture: cross-generation pairing benefits GRU4Rec and SASRec, whereas FMLP initially favors within-generation pairing and shifts toward cross-generation pairing after a second update. Rank separation selects the stronger family in 12/12 first-update and 5/6 second-update dataset-encoder settings; on held-out tests, the selected family outperforms the direct successor in 34/36 trajectories. Five transfer mechanisms do not consistently reproduce these gains in one model. These findings establish state retention as a distinct Rec-RSI problem: progress may reside in relations between generations as well as in the latest model. Code is available at \href{https://github.com/Jinfeng-Xu/RecRSI}{https://github.com/Jinfeng-Xu/RecRSI}.

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 检索器升级通常只用聚合指标（如nDCG、Recall）评估，这类平均指标会掩盖在旧检索器已正确服务的查询上发生的退化。作者希望引入"负向翻转"这一查询级视角，量化因替换检索器而丢失的已成功查询，从而暴露聚合指标看不见的兼容性风险。

Negative-flip rates are lower in the larger-depth settings, where the BM25-supported cohort is defined separately at each depth. These rates use the any-relevant support label. On HotpotQA at k=10, requiring every positive qrel passage raises the negative-flip rate to 12.3-17.3%. Simple fixed-budget combinations with BM25 reduce these regressions, and a HotpotQA reader experiment provides a limited downstream check in which some retrieval flips are accompanied by answer regressions. These results motivate evaluating retriever updates using query-level compatibility alongside aggregate retrieval quality.

Our code is available at https://github.com/KevinC2015/Flash.
