### [1] [Verify, Don't Trust: Agentic Model Development for Video Discovery Retrieval at Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21257)
*Hao Fu, Baiting Zhu, Minglei Chen, Yinjie Huang, Shuai Ding*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 搜索引擎/Search Engine | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 提出 EvoPilot，一套人工把关（human-gated）的长周期在线自动研究（autoresearch）方法，通过角色化智能体 + 版本化领域技能 + 类型化适配器执行实验，并用持久化记录与确定性检查保证结论有效性；在视频发现检索系统 VDD 上开展 37 天实验活动，修复评估缺陷后离线提升 3.20pp，7 天在线随机实验取得 GSRR 0.66% 相对提升。

Ranking Relevance: 该工作作用于视频发现检索系统（召回+排序骨架）的实验迭代流程，而非提出新的排序模型结构或特征交互方法，因此对排序算法本身创新有限。但其价值在于：1) 揭示了离线指标（hit-rate）与在线指标（GSRR）脱节时易产生的误归因，对排序模型 A/B 实验设计、评估口径一致性有直接借鉴；2) 大索引下的召回/排序联动实验（如 interaction head）需要在同漏斗、同深度下比较，避免评估深度不一致造成的假信号；3) 持久化经验与确定性检查可嵌入工业级排序模型迭代流水线，降低无效实验与 GPU 浪费。对搜推广系统的实验平台与迭代方法论有中等参考价值。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有 autoresearch 多在分钟级、自包含程序上验证，而真实的在线检索/推荐系统实验是异步系统、小时级变体、周级活动，且会真实影响产品。即使实验跑完，也可能因代码空操作（no-op）、数据窗口泄漏、评估器语义漂移、两组流量走不同服务漏斗等原因得出无效结论。VDD 检索系统的历史案例正是如此：原始 autoresearch 将离线 hit-rate 下降 22pp 错误归因于交互头（interaction head），实为既存评估缺陷。

Method: EvoPilot：1) 人工把关的长周期在线自动研究流程；2) 角色特定智能体，每轮通过版本化领域技能与类型化适配器执行；3) 持久化记录保存实验与失败，确定性检查强制复用已记录的经验教训；4) 研究覆盖 7 个方向，基于每小时刷新的数亿级视频索引；5) 用事后重放（replay）与变异测试（mutation test）校验比较有效性。

Result: 人类把关的验证将 22pp 离线 hit-rate 下降归因于既存评估缺陷（输出深度为 3000 与 600）；修复后匹配比较测出离线提升 3.20pp。事后重放与变异测试拒绝了无效比较、接纳了有效对照；持久化状态恢复了一轮被中断的实验，产物复用节省约 5 GPU 小时。独立的 7 天在线随机实验估计 VDD 切片上 GSRR 相对提升 0.66%。

Conclusion: 长周期在线自动研究的关键瓶颈不在搜索/优化算法，而在实验有效性与工程一致性：no-op、数据泄漏、评估语义漂移、漏斗不一致会系统性污染结论。人工把关 + 确定性校验 + 持久化状态可显著降低无效结论风险，并在真实产品检索系统上获得可信的离线与在线收益，同时节省算力。

Abstract: Large language model (LLM) agents can propose, implement, and evaluate model changes. Autoresearch loops demonstrate this capability through minutes-scale iterations on a self-contained program. Online autoresearch instead spans asynchronous systems, hours-long variants, and weeks-long campaigns that can influence a product. A completed run can still support an invalid conclusion when a code change is a no-op, data windows leak, evaluator semantics drift, or the two arms traverse different serving funnels. We present EvoPilot, a human-gated method for long-horizon online autoresearch. Role-specific agents execute each round through a versioned domain skill and typed adapter. Durable records preserve experiments and failures; deterministic checks enforce recorded lessons.
  We study a 37-day campaign for the retrieval system that powers Video Deep Dive (VDD), an online experience for discovering follow-on videos after a user opens a seed video. The campaign covered seven directions and used an hourly refreshed index of hundreds of millions of videos. Earlier manual experiments had not established a benefit from an interaction head. A primitive autoresearch attempt revisited the direction but incorrectly attributed an offline hit-rate decline of 22 percentage points to the head. We then introduced EvoPilot. Its human-gated verification traced the drop to a pre-existing evaluation defect that produced output depths of 3,000 and 600. After repair, a matched comparison measured an offline improvement of 3.20 percentage points. Post-study replay and mutation tests rejected invalid comparisons while admitting valid counterparts. Durable state recovered an interrupted round, and artifact reuse avoided approximately five GPU-hours. Separately, a seven-day randomized online evaluation estimated a 0.66% relative increase in the VDD slice of Good Search Result Rate for Retention (GSRR).

### [2] [Hybrid GPU-CPU Retrieval for Personalized Search at Ultra-Large Scale](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21281)
*Hao Fu, Jichao Sun, Baiting Zhu, Qiaoling Liu, Yan Shi, Cheng Lu, Liu Liu, Yubo Wang, Xin Yao, Xiangyu Niu, Xu Dong, Wenhan Lyu, Chiyao Shen, Yinjie Huang, Minglei Chen, Shuai Ding, Li Fan, Xiao Kong*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 高

TL;DR: 提出GPU-CPU混合协同serving系统解决"个性化-规模悖论"：GPU路径承载十亿级精排深度，CPU路径覆盖20倍规模库存的轻量个性化打分，两路径按需并行、去重后共享下游排序，已在生产部署并A/B验证。

Ranking Relevance: 直接关联多阶段排序链路：把检索与交互式粗排(pre-ranking)在GPU路径上融合，是典型的"召回-粗排一体化"设计，可减少级联损失并让重模型参与粗筛；CPU广覆盖路径本质是轻量个性化召回。两路径候选去重后共享下游排序，构成新的多路召回+统一精排范式。对工业搜索/推荐系统在延迟与成本约束下做异构算力分域排序具有直接参考价值，且结论来自真实A/B，落地可信度高。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 超大规模UGC检索场景下存在"个性化-规模悖论"：全量库存常驻GPU显存资源代价过高，而CPU算力又无法在延迟敏感路径上执行交互密集的深度模型。需要一种在固定延迟与资源预算下同时兼顾深度个性化与广覆盖的架构方案。

Method: 不改模型类，而以编排(orchestration)解决：①高深度GPU路径，在约十亿doc的精选在线池上融合检索与交互式粗排(pre-ranking)；②高广度CPU路径，对约20倍规模的独立在线库存做轻量个性化打分。每次请求可跑单路径或双路径，候选去重后进入共享下游排序。同时给出匹配容量规划以量化GPU承载深度、CPU承载广度的经济性。

Result: 已在生产环境部署。全系统A/B对比旧CPU-only配置，模型相关性指标与实质互动(engagement)均有提升；分路径实验也各自在部署范围内体现正向价值。检索日志显示两路径贡献结构上互异的候选集，serving实测刻画了各自延迟，容量规划验证了深度-广度分工的经济性。

Conclusion: 验证了一套可独立演进的"深度-广度"混合serving架构，适用于超大规模个性化搜索，为在线检索/粗排系统的资源-效果权衡提供了工程化范式。

Abstract: Embedding-based retrieval on user-generated content at the trillion-document scale exposes a sharp conflict between two production demands: deep, expressive personalization for queries with rich user intent, and broad coverage of a massive inventory under fixed latency and resource budgets. We characterize this as the personalization-scale paradox: hosting the full serving inventory in GPU memory is too resource intensive, while CPU compute cannot execute the same interaction-heavy model on the latency-critical path.
  We present a hybrid GPU-CPU co-serving system that resolves the paradox through orchestration rather than a new model class. A high-depth GPU pathway fuses retrieval and interaction pre-ranking over a curated online pool on the order of a billion documents, while a high-breadth CPU pathway searches an independently selected online inventory roughly twenty times larger with lightweight personalized scoring. Either or both pathways can run per request; candidates are deduplicated before shared downstream ranking.
  The system is deployed in production. A full-system A/B test against the legacy CPU-only configuration improves model-scored relevance and substantive engagement, while separate pathway experiments show positive value at their own deployment scopes. Retrieval logs show that the pathways contribute structurally distinct candidates, production serving measurements characterize their latency, and a matched capacity plan quantifies the economic rationale for assigning modeling depth to GPUs and inventory breadth to CPUs. Together, these results validate a practical, independently evolvable depth-breadth architecture for ultra-large-scale personalized search.

### [3] [Auto-Bidding with Disentangled Advertiser Profiles and Train-Free Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21308)

Ranking Relevance: 与排序模型属同一广告系统的相邻环节：auto-bidding 决定竞价进而影响候选与排序结果，画像条件化思想可迁移到排序侧的广告主/上下文条件建模（如双塔或条件化 gate）。其"两阶段解耦+免训练适配"的工程范式对排序/预估模型的在线冷启动与画像更新亦有借鉴价值，但不直接改进排序打分模型本身。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自动出价需为每个广告主提供个性化策略，但广告主竞价行为差异大；profile-based 个性化在推荐等域已被验证，却难以迁移到 auto-bidding，原因是：纯净画像抽取困难、共性/私有信息难以同时建模、画像更新与冷启动适应代价高。

Conclusion: ADAPT 通过解耦广告主画像与免训练自适应，缓解了画像纯净度、共性/私有信息建模与冷启动更新的三重难题，为广告出价系统的个性化建模提供了一套可落地框架。

By characterizing each individual, profile-based methods achieve personalization and have proven effective in domains such as recommendation. Specifically, (i) the stage 1 extracts pure static and dynamic profiles via contrastive learning over the advertiser memory bank; (ii) the stage 2 disentangles the dynamic profile into a common profile and a private profile, and combines them with the static profile to jointly condition the bidding strategy; (iii) once trained, ADAPT constructs profiles for new advertisers and updates profiles of existing advertisers without retraining. Our experiments on a large-scale auto-bidding benchmark demonstrate that ADAPT consistently achieves superior performance, and ablation studies further validate the effectiveness of each module. The source code will be released at https://github.com/YuzunoKawori/ADAPT.

### [5] [Predictable Failure in Multi-Hop Retrieval: Score-Distributional Confidence Scoring and Abstention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22056)
*Andre Bacellar*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 中

TL;DR: 论文证明多跳检索失败在结构可预测的子群体中聚集，提出两个理论结果并实例化为 RegimeAbstain 方法，用检索置信分数(RCS)实现校准弃答策略，定义 CWAR 指标，在三个多跳基准和两种检索架构上取得最佳 AUC-AC。

Ranking Relevance: 该工作聚焦多跳检索(RAG 场景)的置信度估计与弃答策略，与排序模型的直接关联较弱，但对工业搜推广系统有两点借鉴价值：(1) "失败机制聚集 + 特征机制互补"的思路可迁移到召回/排序阶段的置信度建模与选择性预测，例如在粗排/精排中对低置信样本弃答或降权；(2) 无需额外大模型调用的轻量结构特征(查询长度、跳数集中度等)构建校准分数，契合在线服务的低延迟约束，可用于召回截断阈值自适应或级联系统的早停判断。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 多跳检索失败并非均匀分布在所有查询上，而是集中在某些结构可预测的子群体中。现有置信度方法未能刻画这种失败机制的结构差异，且缺乏对"自信错误"这一关键失败模式的度量。作者希望形式化失败的结构规律，并据此设计无需额外 LLM 调用的弃答策略。

First (CWAR Reducibility): confident-failure reduction is achievable if and only if retrieval features carry mutual information about success, a condition satisfied by LLM-judge pipelines but substantially weaker in dense-only settings, explaining the AUC-AC gap between regimes. Second (Feature Regime Complementarity): no single ANN score feature achieves best predictive performance across all failure regimes; the dominant feature differs between datasets (query length on MuSiQue, hop-1 concentration on HoVer), and a constructive witness pair shows each is necessary in one regime and non-contributory in the other. RCS achieves best or co-best AUC-AC in all five conditions against eight confidence baselines. On MuSiQue (LLM-judge), RCS reduces CWAR from 39.5% to 20.6% at 50% coverage (47.8% relative reduction), with ECE=0.035. A model trained on MuSiQue transfers to 2WikiMultiHopQA with only -0.5pp AUC loss, confirming the domain-agnostic structure of regime features.
