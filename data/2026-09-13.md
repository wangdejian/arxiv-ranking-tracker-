### [1] [When Synthetic Data Hurts: On Catastrophic Forgetting in Skill Retrieval for LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10750)
*Syed Shariyar Murtaza, Yifan Nie, Utkarsh Soni, Eugene Wen, Arvid Frydenlund*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 中

TL;DR: 该论文面向LLM Agent的"技能路由"任务，在34,396个技能库上构建生产级检索路由系统，并研究在真实监督稀缺、依赖合成数据微调时出现的灾难性遗忘问题，验证了多种持续学习式遗忘缓解方法可同时提升OOD与合成ID检索性能。

Ranking Relevance: 技能路由本质上是"查询→技能"的大规模检索与粗筛（recall/retrieval）问题，与搜推广中的向量召回、候选生成高度同构：均面临大规模候选库、稀缺真值、多正例监督。其核心发现——合成数据微调造成OOD灾难性遗忘、需以持续学习正则化缓解——可直接迁移到CTR/CVR预估与召回模型的领域自适应、冷启动与新类目扩展场景，对训练数据构成为合成/仿真数据的工业排序系统具有借鉴意义；reranker部分则对应精排阶段的相似风险。不过其研究对象为LLM技能检索而非经典排序特征交互模型，与排序模型的直接创新关联有限。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM Agent运行期需从大规模技能库中动态检索外部技能，技能选择成为关键瓶颈；但真实监督信号稀缺且为多正例形式，需借助合成数据微调，而合成数据微调会带来对真实与OOD数据的灾难性遗忘，故需要实用的微调配方与基准。

Method: 构建覆盖34,396个技能的生产级技能路由系统；基于有限真实监督+合成数据开展大规模技能检索研究；对比多种受持续学习启发的遗忘缓解微调方法：embedding-anchor正则化、Learning without Forgetting (LwF)、Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC)、L2-initialization；在0.6B Qwen retriever与reranker上评估。

Result: 合成数据微调可提升同分布(ID)检索，但在真实数据与OOD技能上引发灾难性遗忘；所提遗忘缓解方法不仅保持OOD技能检索性能，还使合成ID技能检索在0.6B Qwen retriever与reranker上提升13.98%。

Conclusion: 该工作为监督稀缺、多正例场景提供了实用的基准与鲁棒的微调配方，表明持续学习正则化手段可有效平衡合成数据带来的ID收益与OOD泛化损失，对LLM Agent技能检索与路由系统的工业落地具有直接参考价值。

Abstract: LLM agents increasingly rely on external skills retrieved at runtime, making skill selection from large repositories a critical challenge. We present a production skill router over 34,396 skills and a large-scale study of skill retrieval using limited real supervision and synthetic data. We found that the synthetic-data fine-tuning improves in-distribution retrieval but it causes catastrophic forgetting on real and out-of-distribution (OOD) data. We evaluate several forgetting mitigation fine-tuning approaches inspired by continual learning, including embedding-anchor regularization, Learning without Forgetting (LwF), Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC), and L2-initialization. The results show that these approaches not only retain the performance on OOD skills retrieval but also improve the retrieval on synthetic in-distribution skills by 13.98\% for 0.6B Qwen retriever and reranker. Our results provide a practical benchmark and a robust fine-tuning recipe for scarce, multi-positive supervision.

### [2] [VikingRAG: Accurate and Token-efficient Retrieval-augmented Generation over Structured Documents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11390)
*Peiyuan Gao, Gaoyuan Zhang, Haojie Qin, Yahui Sun, Qianyi Zhang, Yunhao Zhang, Zeyu Wang, Wei Lu*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 中

TL;DR: VikingRAG 是一个目录感知的语义数据管理系统，通过语义与结构访问的紧耦合实现证据缺口驱动的多轮检索，并用经验边复用检索轨迹、自适应升级策略降低 token 开销。

Ranking Relevance: 本文属 RAG 检索侧工作，与搜推广排序模型无直接关系，但存在可迁移的机制性关联：其一，"经验边"对相似查询复用多轮检索轨迹，本质是查询→检索路径的缓存/记忆化，类似检索式推荐与查询重写中的 query-doc 记忆与近邻复用；其二，"自适应升级"是典型的两阶段级联(cascade)思想，与预排序-精排、粗召回-重排中的"低成本决策优先、证据不足才升级"的算力分配策略同源，可迁移到排序中的模型级联与 early-exit；其三，目录感知的结构化检索与层级/向量混合召回相关，对召回阶段的层级化索引与结构化约束召回有参考意义。总体是召回/检索基础设施层面的启发，而非排序建模创新。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有 RAG 方法为获取足够证据而利用文档结构，但结构上下文 token 成本高；多轮交互进一步放大开销。作者希望在保持高准确率的前提下，压缩结构上下文 token 与多轮探索成本。

Method: 1) 目录感知的语义数据管理，将语义检索与结构(目录/层级)访问紧密结合，支持证据缺口驱动的多轮检索；2) 将 agentic 多轮检索轨迹物化为"经验边"，对相似查询复用，避免重复多轮探索；3) 自适应升级策略：证据充分时用一轮经验增强检索直接作答，否则才触发 agentic 多轮检索。

Result: 在真实数据集上，基础系统 VikingRAG 在达到 SOTA 精度的同时仅消耗其 11.6%–51.9% 的 token；加入轨迹复用与自适应升级后，token 成本进一步降至 5.1%–32.5%，并保持有竞争力的准确率与可用的文档存储性能。

Conclusion: VikingRAG 表明结构感知与多轮检索的成本可以通过语义-结构协同、轨迹复用和自适应升级显著压缩，对新兴 AI 知识库具有实用价值。

Abstract: State-of-the-art retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) methods exploit document structures to acquire sufficient evidence, but often incur substantial token costs. To reduce structural-context tokens without compromising high RAG accuracy, we present {\sf VikingRAG}, a directory-aware semantic data management system that tightly integrates semantic and structural access to support structural-context-efficient, evidence-gap-driven multi-round retrieval. To further reduce token overhead of multi-round interaction, we materialize agentic multi-round retrieval traces as experience edges, and reuse these edges for similar queries, avoiding repeated multi-round exploration. To additionally reduce token costs when agentic multi-round retrieval is unnecessary, we introduce an adaptive escalation strategy that answers from one-round experience-augmented retrieval when the evidence is sufficient, and invokes agentic multi-round retrieval only otherwise. Experiments on real datasets show that the base system {\sf VikingRAG} matches high accuracy of state-of-the-art methods while consuming only 11.6\%--51.9\% of their tokens. With retrieval-trace reuse and adaptive escalation, token costs drop to 5.1\%--32.5\% while maintaining competitive accuracy and practical document-storage performance, showing the utility of this work for emerging AI knowledge bases.

### [3] [TimelyRAG: Semantic-Temporal Hybrid Retrieval for Time-Critical Question Answering in Overlapping-Evolving Documents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11572)
*Youngeun Nam, Joeun Kim, Hwanjun Song, Susik Yoon, Jae-Gil Lee, Byung Suk Lee*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 中/Medium

TL;DR: 提出TimelyRAG框架，将时间距离融入检索排序以应对文档"重叠演化"场景，并发布TimelyQABench基准，nDCG@10最高提升28.6%。

Ranking Relevance: 该工作属于RAG检索排序（retrieval ranking）范畴，核心贡献是在排序打分中注入时间距离特征，与排序模型的时间感知/时效性建模直接相关。对工业搜推广的潜在借鉴在于：电商价格、商品描述、活动规则等亦存在"重叠演化"（版本覆盖但内容大部分保留），可在召回/粗排阶段引入版本-时间对齐信号；但其面向法规QA领域的评测依赖于长文档语义重叠，与常规点击/转化排序目标（CTR/CVR）差异较大，迁移需谨慎，故与主流排序模型体系关联为间接相关。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM与RAG在开放域问答中表现优异，但当文档通过修订持续演化时不可靠。现有时间敏感检索方法只针对"独立演化"环境（每次更新为独立快照），而法律、政策、法规常处于"重叠演化"环境：修订覆盖早期条款但保留大部分内容，版本间语义高度重叠，导致难以定位版本适配的文档。

Method: 提出检索器无关（retriever-agnostic）的TimelyRAG框架，将时间距离（temporal distance）引入排序阶段，使查询与版本适配的文档对齐；并构建TimelyQABench，首个面向法规密集型、具重叠演化挑战的基准。

Result: 实验取得一致增益，nDCG@10最高提升+28.6%，表明时间推理对于演化文档上的可靠问答至关重要。

Conclusion: 在重叠演化文档场景下，将时间距离纳入排序是提升RAG可靠性的关键，TimelyRAG与TimelyQABench为法规类时间敏感检索提供了方法与评测基础。

Abstract: Although large language models (LLMs) and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) have advanced open-domain question answering (QA), they remain unreliable when documents evolve through amendments. Existing time-sensitive retrieval methods address only the disjoint-evolving environment, where each update is an independent snapshot. However, laws, policies, and regulations often operate in overlapping-evolving environments, where amendments override earlier clauses while preserving most content, creating strong semantic overlap across versions. We propose TimelyRAG, a retriever-agnostic framework that incorporates temporal distance into ranking to align queries with version-appropriate documents. We also introduce TimelyQABench, the first benchmark for regulation-heavy domains with overlapping-evolving challenges. Experiments show consistent gains, up to +28.6% in nDCG@10, highlighting the importance of temporal reasoning for reliable QA over evolving documents. All resources are available at https://github.com/kaist-dmlab/TimelyRAG.

</details>


### [4] [Your Retriever Already Knows: Distribution-Shape QPP for RAG Retrieval Sufficiency](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11646)
*Matyáš Veselý, Michal Průšek, Jiří Franc*

Main category: cs.IR | Domain: 召回模型/Recall | Importance: 中

TL;DR: 该论文研究RAG中检索充分性的Query Performance Prediction (QPP)，提出24个非词典特征（GeneralQPP），在ViDoRe视觉域上AUROC 0.856、2ms/query，远超本地LLM judge（0.649）且快约3000倍；混合方案在专有SÚJB数据集上达到0.911。

Ranking Relevance: 该工作与排序模型的关联属间接但具启发性：(1) QPP本质上是检索质量的元评估/置信度估计，可作为召回→排序链路中的质量门控信号，用于决定是否触发重排、查询改写或拒答，与工业搜索中"检索是否足够"的判据（如相关性阈值、召回率估计）高度对应；(2) 其基于分数分布形状的特征工程（分数分布偏度、峰度、gap等）与LTR中常用的分数分布特征、pre-ranking/reranking的置信度建模思路一致；(3) 混合"学习特征+LLM judge"的范式与多任务/多信号融合排序框架同构，可作为特征引入排序模型；(4) 但该论文关注RAG检索充分性而非相关性排序本身，未涉及点击/转化等业务目标，对CTR/CVR排序模型的直接贡献有限，更多是召回质量监控与LLM系统鲁棒性的补充方向。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 标准RAG管道在推理时缺乏可靠的检索成功信号，面对歧义或超出范围的查询时生成模型容易产生幻觉。作者受捷克核监管机构部署场景启发（数据敏感性禁止使用第三方LLM API），需要本地化的检索充分性判断方案。

Method: 对比三种QPP范式：(1) 基于分数的特征（GeneralQPP，24个非词典特征，包括15个分布形状、5个查询表面、4个全局特征）；(2) 基于内容的本地LLM judge（Qwen3.5）；(3) 二者混合。在ViDoRe八个视觉域（14,514查询）和专有SÚJB数据集（1,510查询，含500合成对抗样本）上评估，并采用Leave-One-Domain-Out (LODO)验证泛化性及特征剪枝（S1-Lean）。

Result: GeneralQPP (S1) 在ViDoRe上加权平均AUROC 0.856，优于经典QPP文献池（Classic Full 0.835），远超本地LLM judge（0.649，+0.207差距，快~3000倍）。混合方案在ViDoRe上与S1持平（0.863），但在SÚJB上显著占优（Hit@5 AUROC 0.911，对抗检测0.954），代价是LLM延迟。跨数据集排名一致性高（Spearman ρ=0.90）。LODO下S1降至0.706，S1-Lean（13特征逐步子集）恢复至0.719（+0.032 vs 文献池）。

Conclusion: 轻量级非词典评分特征可在极低延迟下有效预测RAG检索充分性，是对本地LLM judge的高效替代；混合方案在对抗场景下更稳健但引入延迟。特征子集剪枝可部分缓解跨域泛化退化。

Abstract: Standard Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines often provide no reliable inference-time signal of whether retrieval succeeded; on ambiguous or out-of-scope queries, generation may then hallucinate. Motivated by a Czech nuclear-regulator deployment where data sensitivity precludes third-party LLM APIs, we compare three Query Performance Prediction (QPP) paradigms for retrieval sufficiency in RAG: score-based features, a content-based LLM judge, and a hybrid. On the eight ViDoRe vision domains (14,514 queries), our 24 non-lexical features (GeneralQPP; 15 distribution-shape, 5 query-surface, 4 global) reach a weighted-average AUROC of 0.856 at 2 ms per query, ahead of a classic-QPP literature pool (Classic Full, 0.835) and well above a local Qwen3.5 LLM judge (0.649, +0.207 gap; $\sim$3000$\times$ faster and cheaper per query). Adding the LLM judgment as one feature (hybrid) matches S1 on ViDoRe (0.863) but gains a statistically significant edge on SÚJB (AUROC 0.911 at Hit@5, adversarial-detection 0.954; 1,510 queries, 500 synthetic adversarial), at LLM latency. Rankings agree across datasets (Spearman $ρ= 0.90$). Under Leave-One-Domain-Out, S1 drops to 0.706; a 13-feature LODO-stepwise subset (S1-Lean) recovers to 0.719 (+0.032 over the literature pool).

</details>
